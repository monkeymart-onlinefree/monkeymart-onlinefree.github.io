What Is an Exponent Calculator?

An exponent calculator evaluates a power of the form aⁿ, where a is the base and n is the exponent. It handles positive, zero, negative, and many fractional real exponents within normal floating-point limits.

How to Use the Exponent Calculator

  1. Enter the base.
  2. Enter the exponent.
  3. Calculate to see the power and scientific-notation output.

Exponent Rules

a^m × a^n = a^(m+n)   |   a^m / a^n = a^(m−n)   |   (a^m)^n = a^(mn)

OpenStax lists the standard product, quotient, power, zero-exponent, and negative-exponent properties. For a nonzero base, a⁰ = 1 and a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ.

Calculation Example

For base 2 and exponent 10, the result is 2¹⁰ = 1,024.

Limitations

Very large powers may overflow ordinary JavaScript floating-point range. A zero base with a negative exponent is undefined. Negative bases with non-integer exponents can produce non-real values and are rejected by this real-number calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a negative exponent do?

A negative exponent represents a reciprocal. For nonzero a, a⁻ⁿ equals 1 divided by aⁿ.

What is a fractional exponent?

A fractional exponent is related to roots. For example, a^(1/2) corresponds to the principal square root when the real-number domain applies.

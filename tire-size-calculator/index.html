How to Read a Tire Size

In 205/55R16, 205 is the nominal section width in millimeters, 55 is the aspect ratio, and 16 is the wheel diameter in inches. The aspect ratio means the sidewall height is 55% of the nominal tire width.

Tire Diameter Formula

sidewall height (in) = width (mm) × aspect ratio ÷ 100 ÷ 25.4
overall diameter = wheel diameter + 2 × sidewall height

What the Comparison Shows

A change in overall circumference changes how far the vehicle travels per wheel revolution. If a replacement tire is larger, actual road speed will be higher than the indicated speed if the speedometer calibration is unchanged.

Compatibility Is More Than Diameter

This calculator only compares geometry. Before changing tire size, verify wheel-width compatibility, load index, speed rating, vehicle clearance, braking and suspension constraints, and the vehicle manufacturer's approved sizes.

Tire dimensions are nominal; real measured dimensions can vary by tire model, rim width, inflation pressure, and load.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 205/55R16 mean?

205 is the nominal tire width in millimeters, 55 is the sidewall height as a percentage of width, R indicates radial construction, and 16 is the wheel diameter in inches.

How is tire diameter calculated?

Sidewall height equals width × aspect ratio. Convert the sidewall from millimeters to inches, double it, then add the wheel diameter.

Can I install any tire with a similar diameter?

No. Diameter is only one compatibility factor. Wheel width, load index, speed rating, vehicle clearance, manufacturer limits, and legal requirements also matter.

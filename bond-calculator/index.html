What Is a Bond Calculator?

Answer: A bond calculator discounts a fixed-rate bond’s coupon payments and face-value repayment using a required yield to estimate the bond’s present price.

How to Use the Bond Calculator

  1. Enter face value and annual coupon rate.
  2. Enter the market yield to maturity and years remaining.
  3. Select coupon payment frequency.
  4. Calculate price and related yield measures.

Bond Pricing Formula

Price = Σ[C ÷ (1+y)^t] + Face Value ÷ (1+y)^n

The calculator uses the periodic coupon and periodic yield based on the selected payment frequency.

Calculation Example

A $1,000 bond paying a 5% annual coupon with a 6% required yield typically prices below $1,000 because its coupon rate is lower than the market-required yield.

Assumptions and Limitations

This is a plain fixed-rate bond model. It does not model callable bonds, floating rates, credit spread changes, accrued interest, taxes, day-count conventions, settlement dates, default risk, or transaction costs.

Bond Calculator FAQs

What is face value?

Face or par value is the principal amount the issuer promises to repay at maturity, subject to credit risk.

What is coupon rate?

It is the stated interest rate used to determine the bond’s coupon payments.

What is current yield?

Investor.gov defines current yield as annual interest payable divided by the bond’s current market price.

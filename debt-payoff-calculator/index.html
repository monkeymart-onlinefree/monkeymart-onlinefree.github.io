What Is a Debt Payoff Calculator?

Answer: A debt payoff calculator estimates how long multiple debts may take to repay when you make all minimum payments and direct extra money using either the highest-interest-rate method or the snowball method.

How to Use the Debt Payoff Calculator

  1. Add each debt balance, APR, and minimum payment.
  2. Enter any extra amount you can pay each month.
  3. Choose highest-interest-rate first or smallest-balance first.
  4. Calculate the estimated payoff timeline and interest.

Debt Payoff Method

Each month, interest is added to active balances, minimum payments are applied, and the remaining payment budget is directed to the priority debt. The priority is either the highest APR or the smallest current balance.

Calculation Example

If three debts require $360 in combined minimums and you add $300 extra, the initial payoff budget is $660 per month. As debts are eliminated, the freed minimum payments remain in the total payoff budget.

Assumptions and Limitations

The simulation assumes fixed APRs, monthly compounding, no new charges, no late fees, and a constant payoff budget. Real credit-card minimums may change as balances change.

Debt Payoff Calculator FAQs

What is the avalanche method?

It directs extra money to the highest-interest-rate debt after minimum payments, which targets the most expensive debt first.

What is the snowball method?

It directs extra money to the smallest balance first, which can reduce the number of open debts quickly.

Which method should I choose?

CFPB presents both methods with different tradeoffs. The calculator shows the mechanics without choosing for you.

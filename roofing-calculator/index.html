What Is a Roofing Calculator?

This roofing calculator estimates the surface area of a simple pitched roof from the horizontal footprint, roof pitch, and eave overhang. It can then convert the area to roofing squares and estimate bundle quantity using your chosen coverage and waste allowance.

Roof Pitch Area Formula

slope factor = √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²)
roof area ≈ projected plan area × slope factor

For example, a 6/12 pitch has a slope factor of about 1.118. A steeper roof has more surface area than its horizontal projection.

Roofing Squares and Bundles

One roofing square is 100 ft². Bundle coverage varies by product, which is why this calculator asks for coverage per bundle rather than assuming every shingle package is identical.

Important Limitations

This simple model does not separately measure hips, valleys, dormers, intersecting roof planes, starter courses, ridge caps, flashing, or complex cut waste. For complex roofs, measure each roof plane and follow product-specific installation requirements.

The output is a planning estimate, not a contractor takeoff. Verify measurements on the actual structure and check local code and manufacturer instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a roofing square?

In U.S. roofing measurement, one roofing square represents 100 square feet of roof area.

How does roof pitch change roof area?

For a simple roof, multiply horizontal projected area by √(1 + (rise/run)²). A 6/12 pitch has a factor of about 1.118.

How much extra roofing material should I buy?

Waste depends on roof complexity, layout, material, valleys, hips, starter strips, and manufacturer requirements. This calculator lets you choose a waste percentage rather than assuming one value.

Sources and Methodology

Calculator.net: Roofing Calculator reviewed to cross-check pitch correction factors and common user inputs. Its 6/12 factor of about 1.118 matches √(1 + (6/12)²).

Geometry method: the slope correction follows the right-triangle relationship between horizontal run and sloped rafter length.

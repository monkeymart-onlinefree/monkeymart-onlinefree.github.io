What Is an Investment Calculator?

Answer: An investment calculator projects how a starting balance and recurring contributions may grow when an assumed return is compounded over time.

How to Use the Investment Calculator

  1. Enter the starting investment.
  2. Add the planned monthly contribution.
  3. Set the investment length and assumed annual return.
  4. Choose whether contributions occur at the beginning or end of each month.

Investment Growth Method

The calculator applies monthly compounding to the balance and adds each contribution according to the selected timing. For a single lump sum:

Future Value = Present Value × (1 + r)^n

Calculation Example

Starting with $20,000 and adding $1,000 each month for 10 years separates the ending balance into total cash contributed and the growth produced by the return assumption.

Assumptions and Limitations

Investment returns are not constant in real markets. This calculator does not model volatility, taxes, investment fees, transaction costs, or sequence-of-returns risk.

Investment Calculator FAQs

Is the return rate guaranteed?

No. The rate is an assumption used to illustrate compounding, not a prediction or guarantee.

Does contribution timing matter?

Yes. A contribution made at the beginning of a period has more time to earn a return than one made at the end.

What is investment growth here?

It is the projected ending balance minus the starting amount and all added contributions.

What Is a Healthy Weight Calculator?

This calculator converts the CDC adult healthy BMI category into a weight range for a given height. For adults, CDC lists a “Healthy Weight” BMI from 18.5 through 24.9.

Healthy Weight Formula

Weight (kg) = BMI × Height (m)²

The lower boundary uses BMI 18.5 and the upper boundary uses BMI 24.9. The tool also converts the result to pounds for convenience.

Understanding Your Results

BMI is a screening measure. A person can have a BMI outside the healthy category for reasons that require more context, including muscle mass, body composition, pregnancy, edema, and other health factors.

Limitations

Adult BMI cutoffs should not be used for children or teens. BMI also does not directly measure body fat or fat distribution. Discuss weight and health risk with a qualified healthcare professional when medical decisions depend on the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the healthy weight range calculated?

The calculator rearranges the BMI formula to solve for weight at BMI 18.5 and BMI 24.9 for the height entered.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. CDC describes BMI as a screening measure, not a diagnostic test. It does not directly measure body fat or account for all differences in body composition.

Does this calculator apply to children?

No. Adult BMI categories are intended for adults. Children and teens use age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles instead of adult cutoffs.

Sources and Methodology

Adult BMI category cutoffs are from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). Functionality was cross-checked against Calculator.net’s Healthy Weight Calculator.

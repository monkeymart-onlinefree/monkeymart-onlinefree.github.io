What Is a Currency Calculator?

A currency calculator converts an amount from one currency into another using an exchange rate. In live mode, the result depends on the latest reference-rate data available from the selected data provider. In custom mode, you supply the rate yourself, which is useful for bank quotes, card rates, cash exchange rates, or planning.

How to Use the Currency Calculator

Enter the amount, choose the source and target currencies, then calculate. If you already know the exact rate your bank or exchange service will use, choose Custom rate and enter how many units of currency B equal one unit of currency A.

Currency Conversion Formula

Converted amount = Original amount × Exchange rate. The inverse rate is 1 ÷ exchange rate.

Understanding Your Results

Reference exchange rates are informational and may not equal the final consumer rate. Banks, card networks, money-transfer services, and cash exchangers can apply spreads, fees, or timing differences.

Limitations

Live conversion requires an internet connection. The calculator does not add transfer fees, card foreign-transaction fees, commissions, or taxes unless you include them in a custom rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are live exchange rates the same as bank rates?

Not always. Reference rates are useful benchmarks, while banks and payment providers can add a spread, fee, or different execution time.

Can I use my own exchange rate?

Yes. Custom-rate mode multiplies your amount by the exact rate you enter.

Why does the result change during the day?

Foreign-exchange markets move and data providers update at different times, so the latest available reference rate can change.

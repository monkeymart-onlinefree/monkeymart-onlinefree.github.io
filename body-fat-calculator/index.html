What Is a Body Fat Calculator?

Answer: A body fat calculator estimates what percentage of body weight is fat. This page uses circumference equations associated with the U.S. Navy method and also shows an adult BMI-based comparison estimate.

How to Use the Body Fat Calculator

  1. Enter sex, age, weight, and height.
  2. Measure neck and waist/abdomen with a flexible tape.
  3. Women also enter hip circumference.
  4. Calculate body-fat percentage, fat mass, and lean mass.

U.S. Navy Circumference Equations

Male metric density form: BF% = 495 ÷ [1.0324 − 0.19077 log10(waist−neck) + 0.15456 log10(height)] − 450 Female metric density form: BF% = 495 ÷ [1.29579 − 0.35004 log10(waist+hip−neck) + 0.22100 log10(height)] − 450

Calculation Example

A male with height 175 cm, neck 38 cm, and waist 82 cm produces a circumference-based estimate. Multiplying the percentage by body weight gives estimated fat mass; the remainder is lean mass.

Assumptions and Limitations

Circumference methods are estimates and depend strongly on measurement technique and body shape. The Navy's current operational BCA procedures use official measurement and lookup rules; this page provides formula-based estimation rather than an official service determination.

Body Fat Calculator FAQs

Where should waist measurements be taken?

Measurement procedures differ by sex in classic Navy-style circumference methods; consistent tape placement and relaxed posture are important.

Is the Navy method exact?

No. Circumference-based body-fat values are estimates and can differ from direct or laboratory body-composition measurements.

What is the BMI-based comparison?

It uses the Deurenberg adult equation combining BMI, age, and sex to estimate body-fat percentage.

Use the Percent Off Calculator to find how much you save and the final price after a percentage discount. Enter the original price and first discount, then optionally add a second stackable percentage discount to see the updated sale price and effective discount.

What Is the Percent Off Calculator?

The Percent Off Calculator calculates the amount saved when a percentage is taken off an original price. It also shows the resulting sale price and can account for an additional stackable percentage discount.

It is useful for checking store sales, promotional discounts, coupon combinations, and other percentage-based price reductions before tax, shipping, or retailer-specific adjustments.

How to Use the Percent Off Calculator

Enter the following information:

  1. Original price: Enter the price before any discount is applied.
  2. First discount (%): Enter the first percentage taken off the original price.
  3. Add a stackable second discount: Enable this option if another percentage discount applies after the first discount.
  4. Second discount (%): Enter the additional percentage discount when the stackable discount option is enabled.

The calculator then determines the discounted price, amount saved, and effective discount based on the entered values.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

For one percentage discount:

Savings = Original Price × (Discount ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price − Savings

This can also be written as:

Final Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100)

For example, a 20% discount means you pay 80% of the original price. OpenStax presents the same percent-discount and sale-price relationship.

Variables

  • P = original price, in the selected currency
  • d₁ = first discount percentage
  • S₁ = amount saved from the first discount
  • P₁ = price after the first discount
  • d₂ = optional second discount percentage
  • P₂ = final price after the second discount

For the first discount:

S₁ = P × (d₁ ÷ 100)
P₁ = P − S₁

Stackable Second Discount

A stackable second discount is applied to the price remaining after the first discount, rather than adding the two discount percentages together.

When a second discount is used:

P₂ = P₁ × (1 − d₂ ÷ 100)

The same calculation can be written in one expression:

P₂ = P × (1 − d₁ ÷ 100) × (1 − d₂ ÷ 100)

For example, 20% off followed by another 15% off is not the same as 35% off the original price.

Total Savings

With one discount:

Total Savings = Original Price − Final Price

With two stackable discounts:

Total Savings = P − P₂

Effective Discount

The effective discount compares the total amount saved with the original price:

Effective Discount (%) = (Total Savings ÷ Original Price) × 100

For sequential discounts, the effective percentage is lower than simply adding the two discount percentages because the second percentage applies to a smaller price.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Convert the first discount percentage to decimal form.
  2. Multiply it by the original price to calculate the first savings amount.
  3. Subtract that savings from the original price.
  4. If a second discount is enabled, apply it to the already-discounted price.
  5. Calculate the total amount saved.
  6. Compare total savings with the original price to determine the effective discount.

Worked Example

Suppose an item originally costs $279, has a 20% first discount, and qualifies for an additional 15% stackable discount.

Inputs

  • Original price: $279
  • First discount: 20%
  • Second discount: 15%

Step 1: Calculate the First Discount

Savings = $279 × (20 ÷ 100)
Savings = $279 × 0.20 = $55.80

Price after the first discount:

$279 − $55.80 = $223.20

Step 2: Apply the Second Discount

The second 15% discount applies to $223.20, not $279.

$223.20 × 0.15 = $33.48

Step 3: Calculate the Final Price

$223.20 − $33.48 = $189.72

Final Result

  • Final price: $189.72
  • Total savings: $89.28
  • Effective discount: 32%

Although the advertised discounts are 20% and 15%, the combined effective discount is 32%, not 35%. This sequential treatment matches the standard stackable-discount example used by Calculator.net.

Understanding Your Results

Final price is the amount remaining after the entered percentage discounts have been applied.

Amount saved is the difference between the original price and the calculated final price.

Effective discount shows total savings as a percentage of the original price. It is especially useful when two percentage discounts are stacked.

With a second discount, remember that the second percentage is calculated from the reduced price rather than from the original price.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator focuses on percentage-based discounts. It does not automatically include:

  • Sales tax
  • Shipping or delivery fees
  • Fixed-value coupons
  • Minimum-spend requirements
  • Cashback offers
  • Loyalty rewards
  • Product exclusions
  • Retailer-specific coupon ordering

Actual checkout totals depend on the store's promotion rules.

Retailers may also round currency amounts at different stages of a transaction, which can sometimes create small differences between a manual estimate and the final checkout amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to find the amount saved, then subtract that amount from the original price.

You can also multiply the original price directly by 0.80, because 80% of the original price remains after a 20% discount.

What is the formula for percent off?

The basic formula is:

Savings = Original Price × (Percent Off ÷ 100)

Then:

Final Price = Original Price − Savings
Do I add two stackable discounts together?

No. If a 20% discount is followed by another 15% discount, the second 15% applies to the price remaining after the first discount.

That combination produces an effective discount of 32%, not 35%.

How is the effective discount calculated?

Divide total savings by the original price and multiply by 100:

Effective Discount = (Total Savings ÷ Original Price) × 100

This converts the complete savings from all applied discounts into one percentage of the original price.

Does the calculator include sales tax?

No. The calculator determines the percentage discount before any separate sales-tax calculation. The actual taxable amount can depend on local tax rules and how a retailer treats discounts or coupons.

Does it include fixed-dollar coupons?

No. This calculator is designed around percentage discounts. A promotion such as "$10 off" is a fixed-value discount and should not be entered as a percentage unless the retailer also provides its equivalent percentage.

Why can the checkout price differ from the calculator?

A retailer may apply taxes, shipping, coupon restrictions, minimum-purchase conditions, loyalty rewards, exclusions, or different rounding rules. The calculator only evaluates the percentage discounts entered.

What is a Percent Off Calculator?

A Percent Off Calculator converts a percentage discount into the amount saved and the final sale price. It is useful for retail sales, coupon codes, services, subscriptions, and any situation where a price is reduced by a percentage.

Percent off formula

Savings = Original Price × (Discount ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price − Savings

The same result can be found by multiplying by the percentage you still pay. For 20% off, you pay 80%, so a $279 item becomes $279 × 0.80 = $223.20.

How stackable discounts work

Successive discounts are multiplied, not simply added. A 20% discount followed by 15% off the reduced price gives $279 × 0.80 × 0.85 = $189.72. The total saving is $89.28, which is an effective discount of 32%, not 35%.

Calculation example

Original price: $279. First discount: 20%. Second discount: 15%. The first discount saves $55.80, leaving $223.20. The second discount saves $33.48 from that reduced price, leaving $189.72.

Limitations

The calculator does not automatically include sales tax, shipping, fixed-value coupons, minimum-spend rules, cashback, loyalty rewards, exclusions, or retailer-specific coupon ordering. Apply those according to the store’s actual terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 25% off $80?

25% of $80 is $20, so the final price is $60.

Are 50% off and then 50% off free?

No. The second 50% is applied to the already-halved price, so you pay 25% of the original price—an effective 75% discount.

Can the discount be 100%?

Yes. A 100% discount reduces the remaining eligible price to zero.

Does this include tax?

No. It calculates the discount itself. Tax rules differ by location and may apply before or after certain promotions.

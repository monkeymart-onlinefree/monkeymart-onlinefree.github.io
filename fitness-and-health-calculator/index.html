What Is a Fitness & Health Calculator?

Answer: This dashboard combines several adult screening and energy-estimation calculations: BMI, Mifflin–St Jeor BMR, activity-adjusted maintenance calories, a BMI-based healthy-weight range, and an adult BMI-based body-fat estimate.

How to Use the Fitness & Health Calculator

  1. Enter sex, age, weight, and height.
  2. Select an activity level.
  3. Calculate to see multiple related health estimates.
  4. Use dedicated calculators when you need more detailed inputs or methodology.

Health Metric Formulas

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)² BMR (men) = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5 BMR (women) = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161 Maintenance calories ≈ BMR × activity factor

Calculation Example

A 30-year-old adult weighing 70 kg at 175 cm has a BMI near 22.9. BMR then depends on the sex-specific Mifflin–St Jeor constant, and maintenance calories depend on the chosen activity factor.

Assumptions and Limitations

BMI is a screening measure, activity multipliers are approximations, BMR equations predict rather than measure resting energy expenditure, and BMI-based body-fat equations have individual error.

Fitness & Health Calculator FAQs

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. CDC describes adult BMI as a screening measure that should be considered with other health information.

What is BMR?

Basal metabolic rate is an estimate of energy required to support basic body functions at rest.

Are maintenance calories exact?

No. They are estimates; actual energy needs vary with activity, body composition, physiology, and measurement error.

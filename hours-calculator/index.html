What Is an Hours Calculator?

Answer: An hours calculator finds the elapsed time between two clock times and can express that interval in hours and minutes, decimal hours, minutes, or seconds.

How to Use the Hours Calculator

  1. Enter the start time.
  2. Enter the end time.
  3. If the interval crosses midnight, enable the overnight option.
  4. Calculate the elapsed hours.

Hours Between Times

Elapsed minutes = end clock minutes − start clock minutes Decimal hours = elapsed minutes ÷ 60

Calculation Example

From 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.50 decimal hours.

Assumptions and Limitations

This page does not subtract unpaid breaks, overtime rules, or workday policies. Use a time-card/payroll tool when those rules matter.

Hours Calculator FAQs

What are decimal hours?

Decimal hours express minutes as a fraction of 60; 30 minutes equals 0.50 hour.

Can it calculate an overnight shift?

Yes. Enable the next-day option when the end time is earlier than the start time.

Does it calculate wages?

No. This page only measures elapsed time.

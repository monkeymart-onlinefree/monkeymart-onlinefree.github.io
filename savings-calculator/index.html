What Is a Savings Calculator?

A savings calculator estimates how an initial deposit and recurring contributions may grow over time. It combines contributions with compound interest and can also model annual contribution increases and tax on interest. The projection is useful for goals such as an emergency fund, home deposit, education, travel, or retirement savings.

How to Use the Savings Calculator

Enter your starting balance, monthly and annual contributions, expected contribution growth, annual interest rate, saving period, and any tax rate you want applied to interest. The results show your projected ending balance, total deposits, interest earned, and a year-by-year accumulation table.

Savings Formula

Balance next month = Current balance × (1 + monthly after-tax rate) + monthly contribution

The monthly rate is derived from the entered annual effective rate. This page uses a month-by-month simulation so changing contributions and tax can be handled consistently.

Formula Variables

Initial deposit is the starting principal. Contribution is new money added. Interest rate is the assumed annual effective return. Tax rate reduces interest in this simplified model. Time controls the number of compounding periods.

Calculation Example

If you start with $20,000, add $300 each month plus $1,000 each year, and earn a 5% annual effective return for 10 years, the calculator compounds the balance monthly while adding contributions at the stated timing. Raising contributions each year increases the projected balance.

Understanding Your Results

Total deposits show how much money you contributed. Interest earned is the difference between the ending balance and total deposits. The year table makes it easier to see when compounding begins to contribute a larger share of growth.

Limitations

Actual savings rates can change, taxes can be more complex, fees may apply, and investment returns can be volatile. This estimate does not guarantee future performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What interest rate should I use?

Use the annual percentage yield or an estimated effective annual return that fits the account or savings product you are modeling. A guaranteed bank rate and an investment return are not the same thing.

Are contributions made at the beginning or end of the month?

This calculator adds monthly contributions at the end of each month and annual contributions at the end of each year. That assumption is stated so the result can be reproduced.

Does the result include tax on interest?

Yes, if you enter a tax rate. The model reduces each period’s interest by the entered tax percentage. Actual tax treatment depends on the account and jurisdiction.

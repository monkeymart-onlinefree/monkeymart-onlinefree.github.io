What Is an Area Calculator?

An area calculator finds the amount of two-dimensional space inside a plane shape. This page supports several common shapes and applies the corresponding standard geometry formula to dimensions entered in one consistent unit.

How to Use the Area Calculator

  1. Select a shape.
  2. Enter all requested dimensions in the same unit.
  3. Choose a unit label and calculate. The result is shown in square units.

Calculation Example

A rectangle 5 meters long and 4 meters wide has area 5 × 4 = 20 m².

Limitations

The formulas assume ideal geometric shapes and consistent dimensions. Irregular regions may require decomposition, coordinate methods, numerical integration, or direct measurement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are area units squared?

Area measures two dimensions. Multiplying one length by another gives a squared unit, such as m² or ft².

What radius should I use for a circle?

Use the distance from the center to the edge. If you know the diameter instead, divide it by 2 to get the radius.

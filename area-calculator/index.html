The Area Calculator finds the area of common 2D shapes, including rectangles, squares, triangles, circles, parallelograms, trapezoids, ellipses, and rhombi. Select a shape, use a consistent measurement unit, enter the dimensions required for that shape, and the calculator returns the area in square units.

What Is the Area Calculator?

An area calculator measures the amount of two-dimensional space inside a geometric shape. It is useful for geometry problems and practical measurements such as floors, walls, plots, panels, surfaces, and other regularly shaped regions.

The calculation method changes with the selected shape because each shape has its own area formula.

How to Use the Area Calculator

  1. Select the Shape: Choose the geometric shape you want to calculate, such as a rectangle, square, triangle, circle, parallelogram, trapezoid, ellipse, or rhombus.
  2. Choose the Unit: Select the length unit that matches your measurements. All dimensions used in the same calculation should use a consistent unit.
  3. Enter the Required Dimensions: Provide the measurements requested for the selected shape, such as length, width, side, base, height, radius, semi-axes, or diagonals.
  4. Calculate: The calculator applies the relevant geometry formula and returns the area.

If the measurements are in meters, the area is expressed in square meters (m²). Measurements in centimeters produce square centimeters (cm²), and the same rule applies to other consistent length units.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formulas

The calculator uses the area formula that corresponds to the selected shape.

Shape Area Formula
Rectangle A = l × w
Square A = s²
Triangle A = ½bh
Circle A = πr²
Parallelogram A = bh
Trapezoid A = ½(a + b)h
Ellipse A = πab
Rhombus A = ½d₁d₂ when the diagonals are used

These are standard plane-geometry area relationships. OpenStax covers the common rectangle, square, triangle, circle, parallelogram, and trapezoid formulas, while mathematics references from LibreTexts support the ellipse and diagonal-based rhombus formulas.

Variables

A = area, expressed in square units.

l = rectangle length.

w = rectangle width.

s = side length of a square.

b = base of a triangle or parallelogram.

h = perpendicular height.

r = radius of a circle.

For a trapezoid, a and b are the lengths of the two parallel sides, while h is the perpendicular distance between them.

For an ellipse, a and b represent its two semi-axes.

For a rhombus calculated from diagonals, d₁ and d₂ are the lengths of the two diagonals.

All length measurements should use the same unit before applying a formula.

Calculation Sequence

The calculation follows a simple shape-based process:

  1. Identify the selected shape.
  2. Read the dimensions required for that shape.
  3. Apply the corresponding area formula.
  4. Perform the multiplication, squaring, or π-based calculation.
  5. Express the result in the square of the selected length unit.

For example, a circle entered in centimeters produces an area in cm² because its radius is squared in the formula.

What Is a Loan Calculator?

Answer: A loan calculator estimates the periodic payment and interest cost of an amortized fixed-rate loan. Each payment contains interest on the remaining balance plus principal that reduces the balance.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the amount borrowed.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Set the loan term in years and months.
  4. Calculate the monthly payment and review the amortization schedule.

Fixed Loan Payment Formula

Payment = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

For a zero-interest loan, payment is simply principal divided by the number of payments.

Calculation Example

A $25,000 loan over 5 years at 8% is repaid across 60 monthly installments. Early payments contain more interest because the outstanding balance is higher.

Assumptions and Limitations

The estimate assumes a fixed rate, regular monthly payments, and no fees, missed payments, prepayment penalties, or changing rates.

Loan Calculator FAQs

What is an amortized loan?

An amortized loan is repaid through scheduled payments that cover interest and gradually reduce principal.

Why does interest fall over time?

Monthly interest is calculated on the remaining balance, so it usually declines as principal is repaid.

Does this calculator include fees?

No. Add lender fees separately when comparing real loan offers.

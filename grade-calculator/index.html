Use this Grade Calculator to calculate a weighted course grade from scored coursework and estimate the score needed on the remaining course weight to reach a target final grade. It uses each score together with its assigned weight to calculate the result.

What Is the Grade Calculator?

The Grade Calculator helps you understand your current weighted performance and plan for remaining coursework. It is useful when assignments, quizzes, projects, or exams contribute different percentages toward the final course grade.

You can also enter a target final grade and the percentage of the course still remaining to estimate the average score required on that remaining work.

How to Use the Grade Calculator

Add the coursework rows used in your calculation, including the score and weight for each graded item.

Then use these planning inputs:

  • Target final grade (%): Enter the overall course percentage you want to finish with.
  • Remaining course weight (%): Enter the percentage of the final course grade that has not yet been completed.

Make sure the weights match the grading structure in your course syllabus.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

The calculator uses the weighted-average formula:

G=∑(si×wi)∑wiG=\frac{\sum(s_i\times w_i)}{\sum w_i}

Where:

  • G = weighted grade
  • sᵢ = score for an individual graded item, in percent
  • wᵢ = weight assigned to that item
  • Σ = sum of all included values

A weighted grade gives higher-weight coursework a greater effect on the result than lower-weight coursework. University grading guidance uses the same general approach of multiplying grades by their respective weights and combining the weighted values.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Multiply each entered score by its corresponding weight.
  2. Add all weighted score values.
  3. Add all weights entered.
  4. Divide the total weighted score by the total weight entered.
  5. For final-grade planning, compare the grade points already earned with the remaining course weight.
  6. Solve for the score required on the remaining work to reach the target final grade.

Weight Entered

The total weight entered is:

W=∑wiW=\sum w_i

For example, if the entered coursework has weights of 30%, 20%, and 20%, the total weight entered is:

30+20+20=70%30+20+20=70\\%

Needed Score on Remaining Work

When course weights total 100%, the required score on the remaining coursework can be found by rearranging the weighted-grade relationship:

N=100T−ERN=\frac{100T-E}{R}

Where:

  • N = percentage needed on remaining work
  • T = target final grade percentage
  • E = sum of score × weight for completed coursework
  • R = remaining course weight in percentage points

This calculation assumes the remaining weight correctly represents the part of the course that has not yet been completed.

Worked Example

Suppose a student has these completed grades:

  • Exam: 90% with a 30% course weight
  • Project: 82% with a 20% course weight
  • Quizzes: 92% with a 20% course weight
  • Target final grade: 85%
  • Remaining course weight: 30%

Step 1: Calculate Weighted Grade Points

90×30=270090\times30=2700 82×20=164082\times20=1640 92×20=184092\times20=1840

Total:

2700+1640+1840=61802700+1640+1840=6180

Step 2: Calculate the Current Weighted Grade

Total weight entered:

30+20+20=7030+20+20=70

Current weighted grade:

618070=88.2857%\frac{6180}{70}=88.2857\\%

So the weighted grade for the entered coursework is approximately 88.29%.

Step 3: Calculate the Score Needed on Remaining Work

The student wants an 85% final grade with 30% of the course remaining:

N=(100×85)−618030N=\frac{(100\times85)-6180}{30} N=8500−618030N=\frac{8500-6180}{30} N=232030=77.3333%N=\frac{2320}{30}=77.3333\\%

Final Result

The student needs an average of approximately 77.33% on the remaining 30% of the course to finish with an 85% overall grade.

Understanding Your Results

Weighted Course Grade

This percentage represents the weighted average of the coursework currently included in the calculator. Higher-weight items affect this result more strongly.

Weight Entered

Weight entered shows how much course weight is represented by the coursework included in the calculation. If it shows 70%, the entered coursework represents 70% of the course weighting.

Needed on Remaining Work

This result estimates the average percentage required across the remaining course weight to reach your selected target final grade.

A result above 100% means the target would require more than full marks on the remaining work under the basic weighted-grade model. A result below 0% means the entered grades already mathematically provide enough weighted points to reach the selected target even with no additional points from the remaining weight.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculation assumes that the scores and weights you enter match the grading structure used by your course.

Actual final grades may differ when an instructor or institution uses:

  • Extra credit
  • Dropped assignments
  • Category caps
  • Curved grades
  • Special rounding rules
  • Pass/fail requirements
  • Different grading or GPA policies

The weighted-grade calculation does not automatically determine institution-specific grading policies. Check your course syllabus or official gradebook when those rules apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a weighted course grade calculated?

Each score is multiplied by its weight. The weighted values are added together and divided by the total weight entered.

Do my entered weights have to equal 100%?

Not necessarily for calculating the weighted average of the coursework already entered. The formula divides by the total weight included. However, final-grade planning requires the remaining course weight to correctly represent the portion of the complete course that is still unfinished.

What does Weight Entered mean?

It is the combined weight of the coursework currently included in the calculation. Three items weighted 30%, 20%, and 20% represent 70% of the course.

How does the calculator determine what I need on my remaining work?

It calculates how many weighted grade points are still required to reach your target and divides that amount by the remaining course weight.

What if the required grade is higher than 100%?

Under the simple weighted model, a result above 100% means the selected target cannot be reached through normal full-credit scores on the remaining coursework alone. Extra credit or special course policies could change the actual outcome.

Does the calculator include dropped assignments or extra credit?

The standard weighted calculation does not automatically account for special rules such as dropped scores, extra credit, grade curves, or category caps. Those rules can change the final course grade.

Is a weighted course grade the same as GPA?

No. A weighted course grade calculates performance within a course based on assignment weights. GPA usually combines grade-point values across multiple courses, often using course credits as weights.

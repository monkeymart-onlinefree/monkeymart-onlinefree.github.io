What Is a Grade Calculator?

A grade calculator combines assignment or exam scores according to their weights. It is useful when a course grade is based on categories or assessments that contribute different percentages to the final mark.

Weighted Grade Formula

Weighted grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weights). For final-grade planning, the calculator compares the grade points already earned with the course weight still remaining.

Letter Grades

Letter-grade cutoffs vary by school, instructor, and country. The reference table on this page shows one common U.S. mapping similar to the example used by Calculator.net, but your syllabus should take priority.

Limitations

Extra credit, dropped assignments, category caps, curved grading, pass/fail rules, and institution-specific GPA policies can change the final result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do weighted grades work?

Each score contributes in proportion to its weight. A 40%-weight exam affects the final grade more than a 10%-weight quiz.

What if my weights do not add to 100%?

The current-grade result normalizes the weights you entered. Final-grade planning assumes the remaining course weight you specify completes the course structure.

Are letter-grade cutoffs universal?

No. Schools and instructors may use different boundaries, so always check the official syllabus or grading policy.

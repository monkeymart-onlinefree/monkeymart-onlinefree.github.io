What Is a Slope Calculator?

A slope calculator finds the steepness and direction of the line through two points. From (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂), it also derives the rise, run, line equation when defined, inclination angle, and straight-line distance.

How to Use the Slope Calculator

  1. Enter x and y for the first point.
  2. Enter x and y for the second point.
  3. Calculate to see the slope and related line information.

Slope Formula

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)

The numerator is the rise and the denominator is the run. OpenStax gives this two-point slope formula along with point-slope and related line equations.

Calculation Example

For (1,2) and (3,6), rise = 4 and run = 2, so m = 4/2 = 2. The line is y = 2x.

Limitations

If both points are identical, they do not define a unique line. If x₁ = x₂ but y values differ, the line is vertical and its slope is undefined.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a negative slope mean?

A negative slope means y decreases as x increases when moving from left to right.

How is the angle calculated?

For a nonvertical line, the inclination angle shown here is arctan(m) converted from radians to degrees.

What Is a Macro Calculator?

Answer: A macro calculator converts a daily calorie target into grams of protein, carbohydrate, and fat based on the percentage of calories assigned to each macronutrient.

How to Use the Macro Calculator

  1. Enter your daily calorie target.
  2. Choose the percentage of calories from protein, carbohydrate, and fat.
  3. Make sure the percentages total 100%.
  4. Calculate the grams per day and review the adult AMDR check.

Macronutrient Conversion Formulas

Protein grams = calories × protein% ÷ 4 Carbohydrate grams = calories × carbohydrate% ÷ 4 Fat grams = calories × fat% ÷ 9

Protein and carbohydrate provide about 4 kcal per gram; fat provides about 9 kcal per gram.

Calculation Example

At 2,200 kcal with 25% protein, 45% carbohydrate, and 30% fat, the calculator converts each calorie share into grams using the standard energy-per-gram factors.

Assumptions and Limitations

Macro percentages are not a personalized diet prescription. Needs vary with age, life stage, disease, athletic goals, food quality, total energy needs, and professional recommendations.

Macro Calculator FAQs

What are adult AMDRs?

National Academies references commonly list adult AMDRs of 10–35% of calories from protein, 45–65% from carbohydrate, and 20–35% from fat.

Why divide protein and carbs by 4?

Protein and carbohydrate provide about 4 kilocalories per gram, so calorie allocations can be converted to grams by dividing by 4.

Why divide fat by 9?

Fat provides about 9 kilocalories per gram.

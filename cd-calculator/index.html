What Is a CD Calculator?

Answer: A CD calculator estimates the future value of a certificate of deposit when interest is compounded at a stated frequency. It can also illustrate a user-entered early-withdrawal penalty.

How to Use the CD Calculator

  1. Enter the deposit amount.
  2. Enter the nominal annual rate and compounding frequency.
  3. Enter the term.
  4. Optionally enter the bank’s early-withdrawal penalty in months of interest.

CD Compound Interest Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) APY = (1 + r/n)^n − 1

Calculation Example

A $10,000 CD at a 4.5% nominal rate compounded monthly for two years grows through periodic compounding. The effective APY is slightly above the nominal rate.

Assumptions and Limitations

Actual CD disclosures may quote APY directly, use different day-count rules, offer callable or variable-rate features, or define early-withdrawal penalties differently. The penalty field here is a simplified estimate.

CD Calculator FAQs

What is a CD?

FDIC describes a certificate of deposit as a deposit account that generally requires funds to remain for a stated period or an early-withdrawal penalty may apply.

What is APY?

APY expresses the effective annual growth after compounding.

Is an early-withdrawal penalty always the same?

No. Penalties vary by institution and product, so use the actual CD disclosure.

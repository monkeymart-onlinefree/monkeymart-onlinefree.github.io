What Is a Speed Calculator?

This calculator solves the standard speed–distance–time relationship. Provide any two known quantities and select the third quantity to calculate. It also converts common speed units such as meters per second, kilometers per hour, miles per hour, knots, and feet per second.

Speed, Distance, and Time Formula

speed = distance ÷ time
distance = speed × time
time = distance ÷ speed

Example

If a vehicle covers 100 km in 2 hours, its average speed is 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h. This is an average over the whole interval; actual instantaneous speed can vary during the trip.

Unit Conversion

The calculator converts speed through meters per second. NIST lists 1 km/h as approximately 0.2777778 m/s and 1 mph as exactly 0.44704 m/s under standard unit definitions.

This calculator assumes consistent average motion over the measured distance and time. Stops, acceleration, route changes, and measurement error can make real-world travel differ.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for speed?

Speed equals distance divided by time. Rearranging the same relationship gives distance = speed × time and time = distance ÷ speed.

How do I convert km/h to m/s?

Multiply kilometers per hour by 0.277777… (or divide by 3.6). For example, 36 km/h equals 10 m/s.

Is speed the same as velocity?

Speed is a scalar magnitude. Velocity includes direction as well as magnitude, so velocity is a vector quantity.

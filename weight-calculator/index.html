What Is a Weight Calculator?

A weight calculator on this page is a unit converter for mass. Enter a value, choose the starting unit and target unit, and the calculator converts through kilograms as a common reference unit. It is useful for cooking, shipping, exercise logs, science work, and international measurements.

How Weight Conversion Works

Each supported unit is assigned a factor relative to one kilogram. The input is first converted to kilograms, then divided by the target unit's kilogram factor.

target value = input value × (from-unit kg factor ÷ to-unit kg factor)

Example

For 70 kg to pounds, the exact international pound definition gives 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg. Therefore 70 ÷ 0.45359237 ≈ 154.324 lb.

Mass vs. Weight

Strictly, mass and weight are different physical quantities. Mass is measured in kilograms, while weight is a force and can be expressed in newtons. Everyday “weight” conversions usually mean mass, so this calculator follows that common usage.

Methodology: the pound-to-kilogram relationship follows the international avoirdupois definition. Other customary units are derived consistently from pounds or kilograms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is weight the same as mass?

In everyday use, weight and mass are often treated as the same. In physics, mass measures the amount of matter while weight is a force caused by gravity. This converter handles common mass units used for everyday “weight” measurements.

How many kilograms are in one pound?

One international avoirdupois pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilogram. The converter uses that exact relationship for pound-to-kilogram conversions.

How many ounces are in a pound?

There are exactly 16 avoirdupois ounces in one avoirdupois pound.

What Is a Mortgage Calculator?

Answer: A mortgage calculator estimates the payment on a home loan from the loan amount, interest rate, and term. This version also adds user-entered property tax, homeowners insurance, and HOA costs to estimate a broader monthly housing payment.

How to Use the Mortgage Calculator

  1. Enter the home price and down payment.
  2. Enter the fixed interest rate and loan term.
  3. Add estimated annual property tax, insurance, and monthly HOA dues.
  4. Calculate the estimated monthly payment.

Mortgage Payment Formula

The standard fixed-rate principal-and-interest payment is:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments.

Calculation Example

For a $400,000 home with an $80,000 down payment, the financed principal is $320,000. A 30-year fixed loan at 6.5% produces a principal-and-interest payment, then the calculator adds the entered tax, insurance, and HOA estimates.

Assumptions and Limitations

This is not a lender quote. It does not automatically calculate PMI, escrow adjustments, closing costs, points, changing tax bills, rate changes, or jurisdiction-specific fees.

Mortgage Calculator FAQs

Does the mortgage payment include taxes and insurance?

This page can include user-entered property tax and insurance estimates in the monthly total.

What is principal and interest?

Principal repays the amount borrowed. Interest is the lender's charge for providing the loan.

Why can my lender's payment be different?

Actual payments can include mortgage insurance, escrow changes, fees, and other costs not captured by a simple estimate.

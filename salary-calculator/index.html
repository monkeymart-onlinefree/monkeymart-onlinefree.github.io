What Is a Salary Calculator?

Answer: A salary calculator converts annual gross pay into an estimated take-home amount after selected deductions and taxes. This page is configured as a simplified U.S. 2026 annual tax estimate.

How to Use the Salary Calculator

  1. Enter annual salary and filing status.
  2. Add annual pre-tax deductions.
  3. Optionally enter a simplified state/local income-tax rate.
  4. Select pay frequency to see an estimated paycheck amount.

2026 Salary Calculation Method

Estimated federal taxable income equals salary minus entered pre-tax deductions and the 2026 standard deduction. Federal tax is calculated progressively using the 2026 brackets. Employee Social Security is 6.2% up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500, and Medicare is 1.45%, with an additional 0.9% withholding above $200,000 of wages.

Calculation Example

For an $85,000 salary, the calculator subtracts entered pre-tax deductions and the standard deduction before applying the progressive federal brackets, then adds FICA and any user-entered state/local estimate.

Assumptions and Limitations

This is not a payroll withholding engine or tax return. It omits tax credits, itemized deductions, special deductions, benefits, employer payroll details, state-specific brackets, local rules, and many withholding adjustments.

Salary Calculator FAQs

Are the 2026 federal brackets current?

The page uses IRS-published 2026 standard deductions and tax-rate thresholds current when this calculator was prepared.

Does this equal my exact paycheck withholding?

No. Actual withholding depends on Form W-4 information, payroll timing, benefits, credits, and other factors.

What FICA rates are used?

The employee Social Security rate is 6.2% up to the 2026 wage base, and Medicare is 1.45% with additional Medicare withholding above the applicable wage threshold used here.

FAQ

Common Questions

What Y2Mate-style options were added?

The workflow now uses URL analysis first, then separate Video and Audio tabs, quality choices, thumbnail/title/duration, format labels and individual download buttons. Current Y2Mate-branded sites commonly use this pattern, though their exact features differ by domain.

Does it support high quality?

The interface can show 720p, 1080p, 1440p and 2160p when the source/backend exposes those streams. Some high-resolution streams may require server-side audio/video merging.

Can it download from every platform?

The included backend uses yt-dlp's extractor support for public media. Availability still depends on the site, URL, region, and source restrictions. Private, DRM-protected, or access-controlled media is not intentionally bypassed.

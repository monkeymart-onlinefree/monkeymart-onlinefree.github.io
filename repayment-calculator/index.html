What Is a Repayment Calculator?

Answer: A repayment calculator estimates the scheduled payment and payoff path of a fixed-rate amortizing loan, then shows how recurring extra payments can change time and interest.

How to Use the Repayment Calculator

  1. Enter the current balance and fixed annual rate.
  2. Enter the scheduled repayment term.
  3. Add an optional extra monthly payment.
  4. Compare payoff time and interest with and without the extra amount.

Repayment Formula

Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1] Monthly interest = balance × monthly rate Principal paid = payment − interest

Calculation Example

A $30,000 balance at 7% over 7 years has a scheduled fixed payment. Adding $100 each month reduces principal faster and therefore usually lowers future interest.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator assumes a fixed rate, monthly compounding, no fees, and immediate application of extra payments to principal. Verify lender instructions for extra payments.

Repayment Calculator FAQs

Do extra payments always shorten the loan?

On a standard amortizing loan, extra principal payments generally reduce payoff time when no prepayment restrictions interfere.

Can lenders apply extra money differently?

Yes. Check your lender or servicer instructions to ensure extra funds are applied as intended.

Does this handle variable-rate loans?

No. The model assumes one fixed annual interest rate.

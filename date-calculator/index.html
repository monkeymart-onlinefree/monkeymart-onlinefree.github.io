What Is a Date Calculator?

Answer: A date calculator can measure elapsed calendar time between dates or add/subtract calendar units from a starting date.

How to Use the Date Calculator

  1. Select Between Dates or Add/Subtract.
  2. Enter the relevant dates and units.
  3. Choose inclusive end-day counting if needed.
  4. Calculate the date interval or shifted date.

Date Calculation Method

Elapsed days are based on calendar-date differences. Date shifting uses calendar years and months first, followed by weeks and days, so month lengths and leap years are handled by the browser calendar.

Calculation Example

Adding 30 days to September 1 gives October 1. The days-between mode can optionally include the end date as an extra counted day.

Assumptions and Limitations

This version does not exclude business days or holidays. Calendar month arithmetic near month-end can roll into the following month depending on the target month length.

Date Calculator FAQs

Can I include the end date?

Yes. The checkbox adds one day to the date-difference count.

Does it account for leap years?

Calendar date arithmetic uses the browser's date system, which accounts for leap years.

Does it exclude weekends?

No. This version counts calendar days.

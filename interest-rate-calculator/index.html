What Is an Interest Rate Calculator?

Answer: An interest rate calculator can solve backward from a loan amount, payment, and term to estimate the periodic interest rate that makes those cash flows mathematically consistent.

How to Use the Interest Rate Calculator

  1. Enter the amount originally borrowed.
  2. Enter the fixed monthly payment.
  3. Enter the number of years and additional months.
  4. Calculate the implied nominal annual interest rate.

Interest Rate Calculation Method

The calculator numerically solves the standard amortization equation:

Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

Because r appears in several places, the rate is solved iteratively.

Calculation Example

A $25,000 loan paid with about $507 per month for 60 months implies a nominal annual interest rate close to 8%, subject to rounding.

Assumptions and Limitations

The result is an equation-based rate, not necessarily the legal or disclosed APR. APR calculations can include finance charges, fees, timing conventions, and regulatory definitions.

Interest Rate Calculator FAQs

Is this the same as APR?

Not necessarily. This page solves the rate in a standard payment equation, while disclosed APR can incorporate certain finance charges and regulatory conventions.

Why does the payment need to exceed the zero-interest payment?

For a non-negative interest rate, the payment cannot be lower than principal divided by the number of payments.

What compounding is assumed?

The loan model assumes monthly payments and a nominal annual rate equal to 12 times the solved monthly rate.

The Shoe Size Conversion Calculator converts shoe sizes between US/Canada, UK/India, EU, Japan/Mexico, China, and foot length. Select the category and starting size system, then enter a shoe size or foot length to get approximate equivalent sizes.

What Is the Shoe Size Conversion Calculator?

This calculator provides approximate shoe size conversions across several commonly used international sizing systems.

It can be useful when shopping internationally, comparing shoe labels from different countries, or estimating a shoe size from measured foot length. The conversion is based mainly on foot length and category-specific sizing formulas.

International shoe sizes are not perfectly standardized. ISO 19407:2023 provides conversion tables based on foot length and notes that shoe-size conversions should be treated as guidance because sizing systems developed differently across countries.

How to Use the Shoe Size Conversion Calculator

Category

Choose the appropriate footwear category. The category matters because adult and child sizing systems use different offsets in some regions.

Convert From

Select the size system or measurement you already know, such as:

  • US/Canada
  • UK/India
  • EU
  • Japan/Mexico
  • China
  • Foot length

Size or Length

Enter the known shoe size or foot length. When foot length is used, the calculator converts the measurement into the internal length value required by the relevant sizing formulas.

The result shows approximate equivalent shoe sizes in the supported systems.

Formula / Calculation Method

The calculator uses foot length as the common basis for conversion. This approach is consistent with the principle used by ISO 19407:2023, whose conversion tables are based on measured foot length.

Main Formulas

For adult US/Canada sizes:

US women's size ≈ 3 × L(in) − 21
US men's size ≈ 3 × L(in) − 22

For adult UK/India sizes:

UK/India size ≈ 3 × L(in) − 23

For European sizes:

EU size ≈ 1.5 × L(cm) + 2

For Japan/Mexico:

Japan/Mexico size ≈ L(cm)

For China:

China size ≈ 2 × L(cm) − 10

Child US/Canada and UK/India calculations use different category-specific offsets rather than the adult offsets.

Variables

L(in) = foot length in inches

L(cm) = foot length in centimeters

The conversion method uses inches for the US/Canada and UK/India formulas and centimeters for EU, Japan/Mexico, and China.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Read the selected footwear category.
  2. Identify the selected starting sizing system.
  3. Convert the entered size to an estimated foot length when necessary.
  4. Convert inches and centimeters where required by the target formula.
  5. Apply the formula for each supported sizing system.
  6. Round the result to practical shoe-size increments.

US/Canada, UK/India, and Japan/Mexico results commonly use half-size increments, while EU and China results use whole-size increments in this calculation method. The formulas and increment conventions are consistent with the reference method used by Calculator.net.

Worked Example

Suppose an adult woman has a foot length of 10 inches.

Inputs

  • Category: Adult
  • Convert from: Foot length
  • Foot length: 10 inches

Step 1: US/Canada Women's Size

US women's size ≈ 3 × L − 21
= 3 × 10 − 21
= 30 − 21
= 9

Step 2: Convert Foot Length to Centimeters

10 inches × 2.54 = 25.4 cm

Step 3: Estimate EU Size

EU size ≈ 1.5 × 25.4 + 2
= 38.1 + 2
= 40.1

Rounded to the practical whole-size increment:

EU size ≈ 40

Final Result

A 10-inch adult women's foot corresponds approximately to:

  • US/Canada Women's: 9
  • UK/India: approximately 7
  • EU: approximately 40
  • Japan/Mexico: approximately 25.5
  • China: approximately 41

These values are estimates rather than guaranteed equivalents. Individual brands may label the same foot length differently.

Understanding Your Results

US/Canada Size

This is the approximate US or Canadian size produced from the calculated foot length. Adult men's and women's sizes use different offsets.

UK/India Size

This estimates the corresponding UK/India shoe size from foot length. Adult and child categories use different formulas.

EU Size

European shoe sizing is estimated from foot length in centimeters and rounded to a practical whole size.

Japan/Mexico Size

This sizing method closely follows foot length in centimeters in the calculator's conversion model.

China Size

The China result is estimated using a centimeter-based conversion formula and rounded to a whole size.

Foot Length

Foot length is the common measurement connecting the different sizing systems. ISO's shoe-size conversion standard also uses foot length as the basis for comparing major sizing systems.

Assumptions and Limitations

Shoe-size conversion is approximate. A matching numerical conversion does not guarantee that a shoe will fit.

The calculator does not account for:

  • Foot width
  • Shoe last shape
  • Arch shape
  • Sock thickness
  • Toe allowance
  • Brand-specific sizing
  • Model-specific construction
  • Differences between the left and right foot

Different manufacturers can assign different labeled sizes to footwear designed for similar foot lengths.

ISO 19407:2023 describes its adult conversion information as guidance because international sizing systems have developed and been interpreted differently over time.

For an actual purchase, use the calculated size as a starting point and check the manufacturer's specific sizing chart when available.

Current Shoe Size Standard

As of September 2026, ISO 19407:2023 — Footwear — Sizing — Conversion of sizing systems is the published ISO standard referenced for international shoe-size conversion.

It covers major systems including Mondopoint, European, UK, China, Japan, Republic of Korea, and United States sizing. Its conversion tables are based on foot length and include both adult and children's sizing information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are US men's and women's shoe sizes calculated the same way?

No. In this calculator's adult formulas, US women's sizes use an offset of 21, while US men's sizes use an offset of 22. This produces different labeled sizes for the same foot length.

Is EU shoe size calculated from centimeters?

Yes. The calculator estimates EU shoe size using:

EU size ≈ 1.5 × foot length in cm + 2

The result is then rounded to a practical whole shoe size.

Is Japanese shoe size the same as foot length?

In this calculator's approximate conversion method, the Japan/Mexico result is based directly on foot length in centimeters and is rounded to the supported shoe-size increment.

Why can two brands give different sizes for the same foot length?

Brands may use different lasts, internal dimensions, toe allowances, manufacturing methods, and labeling practices. Shoe-size conversion therefore cannot guarantee identical fit across brands.

Should I measure both feet?

For practical shoe fitting, measuring both feet can be useful because a person's feet may differ slightly in length. The conversion calculator itself converts the size or length you enter rather than modeling differences between individual feet.

Is shoe-size conversion exact?

No. International shoe-size systems do not have a perfect one-to-one relationship. ISO itself describes conversion information as guidance because different systems evolved separately.

Does the calculator account for shoe width?

No. It estimates size primarily from foot length. Width, shoe shape, and brand-specific fit are outside the calculation.

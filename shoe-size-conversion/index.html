What Is a Shoe Size Conversion Calculator?

A shoe size converter estimates corresponding sizes across major labeling systems. This page uses foot length as the common reference because that measurement provides the clearest link between systems, while recognizing that brand sizing is not perfectly standardized.

Approximate Shoe Size Formulas

Adults US women ≈ 3L(in) − 21
Adults US men ≈ 3L(in) − 22
Adults UK/India ≈ 3L(in) − 23
EU ≈ 1.5 × L(cm) + 2
Japan/Mexico ≈ L(cm)
China ≈ 2 × L(cm) − 10

Child formulas use different offsets for US/Canada and UK/India. Output is rounded to common half-size or whole-size increments to make the estimate practical.

Calculation Example

A foot length of about 25.4 cm (10 in) converts to approximately US women's 9, US men's 8, UK 7, EU 40, Japan/Mexico 25.5 after half-size rounding, and China 41 after whole-size rounding.

How to Measure Foot Length

Measure while standing, from the back of the heel to the longest toe. Measure both feet and use the larger measurement. Then compare the result against the manufacturer's chart for the specific shoe model.

Limitations

Size labels do not guarantee fit. This calculator does not model foot width, last shape, arch, socks, toe allowance, brand variation, or model-specific construction. Use conversions as a starting point, not a fitting guarantee.

Sources and Methodology

  • ISO 19407:2023 — Footwear sizing conversion — major-system conversion guidance based on foot length and limitation that tables are guidelines.
  • Calculator.net Shoe Size Conversion — approximate formulas and category behavior reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are shoe size conversions exact?

No. Shoe sizing varies by brand, last shape, width, model, and regional labeling. ISO 19407 describes conversion tables as a guideline because the systems developed differently.

What measurement is the best starting point?

Foot length is the best common anchor for conversion. Measure both feet while standing and use the larger foot, then compare with the specific brand's chart.

Why does the calculator ask for adult men or women?

U.S./Canada adult men's and women's sizing use different approximate formulas. Other systems can also differ in labeling, so category and brand charts matter.

Does this calculator include foot width?

No. It estimates size from length and sizing-system formulas. Width and shoe shape can materially change fit even when nominal length size is the same.

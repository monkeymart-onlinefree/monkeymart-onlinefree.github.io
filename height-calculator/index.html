What Is a Height Calculator?

This page offers two functions: a rough parent-based estimate of a child's eventual adult height and an exact conversion between U.S. customary height units and centimeters.

Parent-Based Height Formula

The American Academy of Pediatrics' HealthyChildren resource describes the mid-parental method. For a boy, add 5 inches to the mother's height, add the father's height, then divide by 2. For a girl, subtract 5 inches from the father's height, add the mother's height, then divide by 2.

Height Conversion Formula

NIST lists 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters and 1 foot = 30.48 centimeters. The converter uses these exact relationships.

Limitations

Adult height is influenced by genetics and environmental factors. The parent-only method is intentionally rough and should not be used to diagnose growth concerns. A pediatric clinician can assess growth patterns using appropriate charts and medical context.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the parent-based height method?

It is only a rough estimate. HealthyChildren notes a wide margin of error, and individual growth can differ for many reasons.

How many centimeters are in one inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimeters.

Can this calculator diagnose a growth problem?

No. Growth concerns should be discussed with a pediatric healthcare professional using age-appropriate growth charts and clinical history.

What Is an Inflation Calculator?

An inflation calculator compares purchasing power across time. For CPI-based calculations, it scales an amount by the ratio of a target Consumer Price Index to a starting index. A flat-rate projection instead assumes the same inflation rate every year and compounds it.

Inflation Calculator Formula

For CPI data: Adjusted value = Original value × (Target CPI ÷ Starting CPI). For a constant annual rate: Future value = Present value × (1 + r)^n.

About the CPI Data in This Page

The built-in quick selector contains recent U.S. CPI-U monthly values. For older periods, use Manual CPI mode with official BLS index values. CPI-U measures price changes for a broad basket of consumer goods and services and does not describe every household's personal inflation experience.

Limitations

Flat-rate inflation is a scenario, not a forecast. CPI data can also be revised or unavailable for a specific month. This calculator does not model taxes, investment returns, wage growth, or changes in spending patterns.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does CPI mean?

CPI is the Consumer Price Index. It tracks the average change over time in prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services.

How do I calculate inflation between two CPI values?

Divide the later CPI by the earlier CPI, then multiply the original amount by that ratio.

Is a flat inflation rate a prediction?

No. It is a scenario that assumes the same annual percentage change repeats and compounds each year.

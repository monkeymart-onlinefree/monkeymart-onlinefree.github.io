What Is the Heat Index Calculator?

The heat index estimates how hot it feels when air temperature and relative humidity are considered together. This calculator follows the U.S. National Weather Service procedure: it begins with a simpler approximation and uses the Rothfusz regression when the preliminary result reaches the applicable range.

How to Use the Heat Index Calculator

Enter air temperature and relative humidity. Choose Fahrenheit or Celsius; Celsius is converted internally to Fahrenheit because the published NWS regression uses °F. The result is shown in both systems.

Heat Index Calculator Formula

HI = −42.379 + 2.04901523T + 10.14333127RH − 0.22475541TRH − 0.00683783T² − 0.05481717RH² + 0.00122874T²RH + 0.00085282TRH² − 0.00000199T²RH²

Formula Variables

T is temperature in °F and RH is relative humidity in percent. NWS also specifies adjustments for very low humidity from 80–112°F and very high humidity from 80–87°F.

Calculation Example

At 90°F and 60% relative humidity, the NWS method yields a heat index of roughly 100°F.

Understanding Your Results

The result is an apparent temperature for the specified model conditions. The calculator also shows the difference between heat index and actual air temperature.

Important Factors

Sunlight, wind, clothing, hydration, activity, acclimatization, and individual health can change actual heat stress.

Limitations

Heat index is not a body-temperature prediction or personal medical threshold. The Rothfusz regression is not valid at extreme combinations outside the data range used to derive it. Follow local heat warnings and safety guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What inputs does heat index use?

The standard NWS heat-index calculation uses air temperature and relative humidity.

Is heat index measured in the shade?

Yes. NWS heat-index values are defined for shady, light-wind conditions. Direct sunlight can make conditions feel hotter.

Why does the formula change near 80°F?

NWS first applies a simpler Steadman-consistent calculation. If the averaged result reaches 80°F, the full Rothfusz regression and any relevant humidity adjustment are applied.

What Is a Commission Calculator?

A commission calculator converts a sales amount and commission rate into earnings. It can also solve backward when the commission or target rate is known. Tiered commission calculations apply different rates to different bands of sales rather than one rate to the entire amount.

Simple Commission Formula

Commission = Sales × (Commission Rate ÷ 100)

To solve for sales, divide commission by the decimal commission rate. To solve for the rate, divide commission by sales and multiply by 100.

How Tiered Commission Works

Each tier is calculated separately. If the first $20,000 pays 3% and the next $30,000 pays 5%, a salesperson with $40,000 in sales earns 3% on the first $20,000 and 5% only on the next $20,000.

Understanding Total Pay

If your plan includes guaranteed base pay, total pay equals base pay plus commission. Real compensation plans may also include bonuses, accelerators, clawbacks, discounts, profit-based rules, or exclusions.

Limitations

This calculator follows the rates and tiers you enter. It does not interpret an employment contract or determine whether a sale is commissionable. Use your official compensation plan for final payroll decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a simple commission?

Multiply the commissionable sales amount by the commission rate expressed as a decimal. For example, 5% of $20,000 is $1,000.

What is tiered commission?

A tiered plan applies different commission rates to different portions of sales. Only the amount that falls inside each tier is multiplied by that tier’s rate.

Is commission the same as total pay?

Not always. If a compensation plan also includes a base salary or guaranteed payment, total pay is the base amount plus earned commission.

What Is a Protein Calculator?

This calculator converts body weight into daily protein grams using a selected grams-per-kilogram range. It always shows the adult RDA reference of 0.8 g/kg/day and can also display higher activity ranges supported by sports nutrition guidance.

Protein Formula

Daily protein (g) = Body weight (kg) × Protein target (g/kg/day)

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that athletes generally require about 1.2–2.0 g/kg/day. The adult RDA reference is 0.8 g/kg/day.

Understanding Your Results

The selected range is a planning estimate rather than a diagnosis. Protein needs depend on age, total calories, training type, training volume, recovery, pregnancy, illness, and clinical conditions. The per-feeding number simply divides the selected midpoint by your chosen number of meals or feedings.

Limitations

More is not automatically better, and medical conditions can require a very different protein prescription. This calculator does not evaluate kidney function, total diet quality, energy intake, or amino-acid adequacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the adult protein RDA?

A commonly cited adult RDA is 0.8 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. Individual needs can differ.

How much protein do athletes need?

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that athletes typically need about 1.2 to 2.0 g/kg/day to support training adaptation, repair, and protein turnover.

Should people with kidney disease use a general protein calculator?

A general calculator is not appropriate for individualized medical nutrition therapy. Kidney disease and other conditions can change protein needs, so a clinician or registered dietitian should set the target.

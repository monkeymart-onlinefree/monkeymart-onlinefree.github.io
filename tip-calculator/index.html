Use the Tip Calculator to calculate a tip from your bill amount and chosen tip percentage. It also shows the total bill and can split both the tip and total equally between multiple people.

What Is the Tip Calculator?

The Tip Calculator is a simple tool for calculating a tip, the final bill after adding the tip, and an equal per-person split.

Enter the bill amount, choose or enter a tip percentage, specify the number of people sharing the bill, and select the currency used for the amounts. The calculator then applies the selected percentage and divides the results equally when more than one person is included.

It can be useful for restaurant bills, delivery services, personal services, and other situations where you choose to leave a voluntary tip.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

Enter the following information:

  1. Bill Amount: Enter the amount on which you want to calculate the tip.
  2. Tip Percentage: Enter or select the percentage of the bill you want to leave as a tip.
  3. Number of People: Enter how many people will split the bill equally.
  4. Currency: Select the currency used for the bill and calculated monetary amounts.

The calculator uses these inputs to determine the tip amount, total bill, tip per person, and total amount per person.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

The tip is calculated as:

Tip = Bill × (Tip Percentage ÷ 100)

The final bill including the tip is:

Total = Bill + Tip

For an equal split:

Total per Person = Total ÷ Number of People
Tip per Person = Tip ÷ Number of People

Variables

  • Bill = original bill amount, in the selected currency
  • Tip Percentage = chosen tip rate, expressed as a percentage
  • Tip = calculated tip amount
  • Total = bill amount plus tip
  • Number of People = number of people sharing the bill
  • Total per Person = each person's equal share of the total bill
  • Tip per Person = each person's equal share of the tip

Calculation Sequence

  1. Convert the tip percentage to decimal form by dividing it by 100.
  2. Multiply the bill amount by the decimal tip rate.
  3. Add the calculated tip to the original bill.
  4. Divide the total bill by the number of people.
  5. Divide the tip by the number of people to calculate each person's share of the tip.

Tip Amount

The tip amount is:

Tip = Bill × (Tip Percentage ÷ 100)

For example, an 18% tip uses:

18 ÷ 100 = 0.18

Total Bill

After calculating the tip:

Total Bill = Bill + Tip

Total per Person

When the bill is shared equally:

Total per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Tip per Person

The individual tip share is:

Tip per Person = Total Tip ÷ Number of People

Worked Example

Suppose a bill is $80, you choose an 18% tip, and 4 people are splitting the bill equally.

Inputs

  • Bill amount: $80
  • Tip percentage: 18%
  • Number of people: 4
  • Currency: USD

Step 1: Convert the Tip Percentage

18 ÷ 100 = 0.18

Step 2: Calculate the Tip

Tip = $80 × 0.18
Tip = $14.40

Step 3: Calculate the Total Bill

Total = $80 + $14.40
Total = $94.40

Step 4: Calculate the Total per Person

$94.40 ÷ 4 = $23.60

Step 5: Calculate the Tip per Person

$14.40 ÷ 4 = $3.60

Final Result

  • Tip Amount: $14.40
  • Total Bill: $94.40
  • Tip per Person: $3.60
  • Total per Person: $23.60

Understanding Your Results

Tip Amount shows how much is added to the original bill based on the percentage you entered.

Total Bill is the original bill plus the calculated tip.

Tip per Person shows how much of the total tip each person contributes when the tip is divided equally.

Total per Person shows each person's equal share of the complete bill, including the tip.

The selected currency provides the monetary unit for the entered bill and calculated amounts.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator uses the exact bill amount and tip percentage you provide. It does not determine what tip percentage is appropriate for a particular country, service, venue, or situation.

Tipping customs can vary by location and type of service. A tip is generally voluntary, while a mandatory service charge may be treated differently.

When multiple people are entered, the calculator assumes the bill and tip are divided equally. It does not calculate separate shares based on individual meals, purchases, or different contribution amounts.

A number of people equal to zero cannot produce a per-person result because division by zero is undefined.

The calculator also does not decide whether the amount you enter should include or exclude taxes, fees, discounts, or an existing service charge. Check the bill before calculating an additional tip.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a tip percentage?

Divide the tip percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the bill amount.

For a 20% tip on a $50 bill:

$50 × (20 ÷ 100) = $10
How do I calculate the total bill after adding a tip?

Add the calculated tip to the original bill:

Total Bill = Bill + Tip

If the bill is $50 and the tip is $10, the total is $60.

How does the calculator split a bill between several people?

The calculator divides the complete bill, including the tip, by the number of people:

Total per Person = Total ÷ People

It also divides the tip by the same number to show the tip per person.

What happens if only one person is entered?

With one person, the total per person is the same as the full total bill, and the tip per person is the full tip amount.

Can I enter a custom tip percentage?

The calculation method works with the tip percentage entered or selected in the calculator. The percentage is converted to decimal form before being multiplied by the bill amount.

Does the calculator tell me how much I should tip?

No. It calculates the percentage you choose. It does not determine an appropriate tipping rate because tipping practices vary by location, service, and circumstance.

What if my bill already includes a service charge?

Check the bill before adding another amount. The calculator applies the selected tip percentage to the bill value you enter; it does not automatically identify or remove existing service charges. The IRS distinguishes voluntary tips from mandatory service charges for U.S. federal tax purposes.

Sources & Methodology

The calculation uses standard percentage arithmetic: the selected tip percentage is converted to a decimal and multiplied by the entered bill amount. Shared-bill results divide the calculated tip and total equally by the number of people.

What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator finds a gratuity from a bill amount and percentage, then adds it to the bill. When a group is paying, it can divide both the total and tip evenly among the selected number of people.

Tip Calculator Formula

Tip = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)
Total = Bill + Tip
Total per person = Total ÷ People
Tip per person = Tip ÷ People

Calculation Example

For a 50.00 bill and a 20% tip, the gratuity is 10.00 and the total is 60.00. Split equally between two people, each person pays 30.00, including 5.00 of the tip.

Tips vs. Service Charges

In U.S. federal tax guidance, the IRS describes tips as discretionary payments and distinguishes them from mandatory service charges. Your receipt may already include a service charge, so review it before adding an extra tip.

Limitations

This calculator does not decide what percentage is culturally or legally appropriate. Tipping customs vary by country, service type, venue, and circumstance. It also assumes an equal split rather than item-by-item sharing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a tip calculated?

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage expressed as a decimal. A 20% tip on a 50.00 bill is 10.00, making the total 60.00.

How do I split a bill with a tip?

Add the tip to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. This calculator also shows the tip share per person.

Is a mandatory service charge the same as a tip?

Not necessarily. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the IRS distinguishes voluntary tips from mandatory service charges. Check the bill so you do not accidentally tip twice unless you intend to.

Should I tip on tax?

Practices vary. This calculator simply applies your chosen percentage to the bill amount you enter, so you can enter the pre-tax subtotal or final amount according to your preference and local custom.

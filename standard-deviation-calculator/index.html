What Is Standard Deviation?

Answer: Standard deviation measures the spread of values around their mean. Population standard deviation divides squared deviations by N, while the common corrected sample standard deviation uses N−1.

Standard Deviation Formulas

Population: σ = √[Σ(xᵢ−μ)² / N] Sample: s = √[Σ(xᵢ−x̄)² / (n−1)]

Assumptions and Limitations

The displayed 95% margin uses 1.96 × standard error as a simple normal-approximation value. Formal inference can require distributional assumptions and t critical values, especially for small samples.

Standard Deviation Calculator FAQs

When should I use sample standard deviation?

Use the sample version when your observations are treated as a sample used to estimate variability in a larger population.

Why does sample standard deviation divide by n−1?

The corrected sample variance uses n−1 degrees of freedom when estimating population variance from sample data.

Can standard deviation be negative?

No. Standard deviation is the square root of a non-negative variance.

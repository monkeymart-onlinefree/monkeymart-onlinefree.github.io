What Is an Auto Lease Calculator?

An auto lease calculator estimates the monthly lease payment from the adjusted capitalized cost, residual value, lease term, financing charge, and tax. It is designed for understanding the mechanics of a standard closed-end vehicle lease estimate.

Auto Lease Formula

Monthly depreciation is (adjusted cap cost − residual value) ÷ months. A common lease finance charge is (adjusted cap cost + residual value) × money factor. When APR is stated as a percentage, an approximate money factor is APR ÷ 2400.

Example

For a $50,000 negotiated price with $8,000 down, $5,000 trade credit, a $25,000 residual, 36 months, 6% APR-equivalent, and 6% tax, the adjusted cap cost is $37,000. The model then adds depreciation, rent charge, and monthly tax.

Key Lease Terms

Capitalized cost is the amount used as the starting lease value after negotiated adjustments. Residual value is the contractual estimate of end-of-lease value. Money factor is the lease financing factor used to calculate the rent charge.

Limitations

Real leases may treat acquisition fees, taxes, rebates, security deposits, first payments, mileage, negative equity, and incentives differently. The CFPB also requires specific lease disclosures. Use the lessor’s actual disclosure form for final payment figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert APR to a money factor?

A common approximation is APR as a percent divided by 2400. For example, 6% ÷ 2400 = 0.0025. A lessor may quote the money factor directly, and contract terms control.

Why does residual value matter so much?

The payment largely covers the vehicle’s expected depreciation during the lease. A higher residual value means less depreciation is being paid for over the lease term, all else equal.

Is a down payment on a lease always a good idea?

A capitalized-cost reduction can lower monthly payments, but it also puts more cash at risk upfront. The financial effect depends on the contract, insurance settlement terms, and your preference for upfront versus monthly cost.

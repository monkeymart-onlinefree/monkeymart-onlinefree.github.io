What Is a 401(k) Calculator?

Answer: A 401(k) calculator projects how an existing workplace retirement balance may grow from employee deferrals, employer contributions, salary changes, and investment returns.

How to Use the 401(k) Calculator

  1. Enter your age, current balance, salary, and years to contribute.
  2. Enter your employee contribution percentage.
  3. Add the employer match formula and expected salary growth.
  4. Choose the applicable employee deferral cap and assumed annual return.

401(k) Projection Method

Each year, the calculator caps employee deferrals at the selected annual limit, estimates the employer match from the match rate and salary cap, adds both to the account, and applies the assumed annual return.

Calculation Example

At a $90,000 salary with a 10% employee deferral and a 50% employer match on the first 6% of salary, the first-year employee contribution is $9,000 and the modeled employer contribution is $2,700 before investment growth.

Assumptions and Limitations

Actual 401(k) plans have plan-specific match formulas, vesting, fees, investment options, Roth/traditional tax treatment, compensation limits, overall contribution limits, and catch-up rules. The IRS can update limits annually.

401(k) Calculator FAQs

What is the 2026 employee 401(k) contribution limit?

The IRS announced a 2026 elective-deferral limit of $24,500 for 401(k) plans.

What is the general 2026 catch-up limit for age 50 and older?

The general catch-up contribution limit for most eligible participants age 50 and older is $8,000 for 2026, although special rules can apply.

Does employer match count against the employee deferral limit?

Employer contributions are not part of the employee elective-deferral limit, but a separate overall defined-contribution plan limit applies.

What Is an Interest Calculator?

Answer: An interest calculator projects how money can grow when interest is compounded and when additional contributions are made. This version also provides simple planning adjustments for tax on interest and inflation.

How to Use the Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the starting amount and monthly contribution.
  2. Enter the nominal annual interest rate and compounding frequency.
  3. Set the investment length.
  4. Optionally enter tax and inflation estimates.

Compound Interest Method

Without additional contributions, compound growth is commonly written as:

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

With recurring contributions, this page compounds the balance monthly using a monthly rate equivalent to the selected nominal compounding frequency, then adds each monthly contribution.

Calculation Example

A $20,000 starting balance with $500 contributed each month grows from both new deposits and compounding. The longer the time horizon, the larger the share of the ending balance that may come from interest.

Assumptions and Limitations

Returns are assumed constant, contributions occur monthly, the tax estimate is simplified, and the inflation adjustment assumes a constant annual rate. Real investments can fluctuate and may have fees.

Interest Calculator FAQs

What is compound interest?

Compound interest means interest can be earned on both the original principal and previously accumulated interest.

What is an effective annual rate?

The effective annual rate reflects the effect of compounding within the year.

Does this predict investment returns?

No. It is a mathematical projection based on the rate you enter, not a market forecast.

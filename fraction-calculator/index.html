What Is a Fraction Calculator?

Answer: A fraction calculator performs arithmetic on rational numbers written as numerators over denominators and simplifies the resulting fraction to lowest terms.

How to Use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter the numerator and denominator of the first fraction.
  2. Choose +, −, ×, or ÷.
  3. Enter the second fraction.
  4. Calculate the simplified, mixed-number, and decimal result.

Fraction Arithmetic Rules

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd a/b × c/d = ac / bd a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

Calculation Example

3/4 + 1/2 = 3/4 + 2/4 = 5/4 = 1 1/4 = 1.25.

Assumptions and Limitations

The inputs on this page are integers. Very large integers can exceed JavaScript’s exact integer range; use specialized arbitrary-precision tools for extremely large values.

Fraction Calculator FAQs

Can a denominator be zero?

No. Division by zero is undefined, so a fraction cannot have a zero denominator.

How are fractions simplified?

The numerator and denominator are divided by their greatest common divisor.

What is an improper fraction?

An improper fraction has an absolute numerator greater than or equal to its denominator and can be written as a mixed number.

Use the Fraction Calculator to add, subtract, multiply, or divide two fractions. Enter the first fraction, choose an operation, and enter the second fraction to get the simplified result, decimal value, and unsimplified fraction.

What Is the Fraction Calculator?

The Fraction Calculator performs the four basic arithmetic operations on two fractions: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

It is useful for checking fraction homework, comparing manual calculations, simplifying fraction results, and converting the final fraction into a decimal value.

How to Use the Fraction Calculator

First Fraction

Enter the numerator and denominator of the first fraction. Both inputs are integers.

For a fraction:

ab\frac{a}{b}
  • a is the numerator.
  • b is the denominator.
  • The denominator cannot be zero.

Operation

Choose the calculation you want to perform:

  • Addition (+)
  • Subtraction (−)
  • Multiplication (×)
  • Division (÷)

Second Fraction

Enter the numerator and denominator of the second fraction:

cd\frac{c}{d}

Both values are integers, and the denominator must not be zero.

When dividing fractions, the second fraction itself must also be nonzero.

Formula / Calculation Method

The calculator applies a different arithmetic formula depending on the selected operation. It first calculates the unsimplified fraction and then reduces the result using the greatest common divisor of the numerator and denominator.

Addition Formula

For:

ab+cd\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}

the calculator uses:

ad+bcbd\frac{ad+bc}{bd}

Subtraction Formula

For:

ab−cd\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}

the formula is:

ad−bcbd\frac{ad-bc}{bd}

Multiplication Formula

For:

ab×cd\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{c}{d}

the formula is:

acbd\frac{ac}{bd}

Division Formula

For:

ab÷cd\frac{a}{b}\div\frac{c}{d}

the calculator multiplies the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second:

ab×dc\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{d}{c}

Therefore:

ab÷cd=adbc\frac{a}{b}\div\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad}{bc}

Variables

Symbol Meaning Unit
a First fraction numerator None
b First fraction denominator None
c Second fraction numerator None
d Second fraction denominator None

For valid fractions, b ≠ 0 and d ≠ 0. For division, c must also be nonzero because the second fraction cannot equal zero.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Read the two fractions and selected operation.
  2. Apply the corresponding addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division formula.
  3. Keep the initial numerator and denominator as the unsimplified result.
  4. Find the greatest common divisor of the result's numerator and denominator.
  5. Divide both by that common divisor to produce the simplified fraction.
  6. Divide the final numerator by the final denominator to obtain the decimal value.
  7. When applicable, the result can also be represented in mixed-number form.

Simplified Result

If the unsimplified result is:

ND\frac{N}{D}

and:

g=gcd⁡(∣N∣,∣D∣)g=\gcd(|N|,|D|)

the reduced fraction is:

N/gD/g\frac{N/g}{D/g}

This produces an equivalent fraction with no remaining common factor greater than 1.

Decimal Result

The decimal value is obtained by dividing the resulting numerator by its denominator:

Decimal=NDDecimal=\frac{N}{D}

Some fractions produce repeating decimals, so the displayed decimal may be an approximate representation of the exact fraction.

Worked Example

Suppose you want to calculate:

34+56\frac{3}{4}+\frac{5}{6}

Inputs

  • First fraction: 3/4
  • Operation: Addition
  • Second fraction: 5/6

Step 1: Apply the Addition Formula

ab+cd=ad+bcbd\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad+bc}{bd}

Substitute the values:

3×6+4×54×6\frac{3\times6+4\times5}{4\times6}

Step 2: Calculate the Unsimplified Result

18+2024\frac{18+20}{24} =3824=\frac{38}{24}

The unsimplified result is:

3824\frac{38}{24}

Step 3: Simplify the Fraction

The greatest common divisor of 38 and 24 is 2.

38÷224÷2=1912\frac{38\div2}{24\div2} = \frac{19}{12}

Final Result

Simplified fraction:

1912\frac{19}{12}

Mixed-number form:

17121\frac{7}{12}

Decimal:

1912=1.583333…\frac{19}{12}=1.583333\ldots

Understanding Your Results

Simplified Result

This is the fraction reduced to lowest terms. The numerator and denominator no longer share a common factor greater than 1.

For example:

1824\frac{18}{24}

simplifies to:

34\frac{3}{4}

Unsimplified Result

The unsimplified result shows the numerator and denominator produced directly by the selected arithmetic formula before common factors are removed.

It can be useful when you want to see the intermediate calculation.

Decimal

The decimal result expresses the fraction using decimal notation.

For example:

34=0.75\frac{3}{4}=0.75

Fractions such as 1/3 have repeating decimal representations, so their on-screen decimal values cannot represent every digit.

Mixed-Number Form

An improper fraction can also be represented as a whole number plus a proper fraction. For example:

1912=1712\frac{19}{12}=1\frac{7}{12}

The improper fraction and mixed number represent the same numerical value.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator accepts integer values for the fraction inputs.

A denominator of zero is invalid because division by zero is undefined. When using the division operation, the second fraction must also be nonzero.

The calculator reduces results to an equivalent simplified fraction using the greatest common divisor.

Decimal representations of repeating fractions are necessarily finite approximations when displayed digitally. For an exact result, use the simplified fraction.

Very large integers can exceed JavaScript's exact integer range. Calculations involving extremely large values may therefore require arbitrary-precision mathematical software.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you add fractions with different denominators?

For two fractions a/ba/b and c/dc/d, the calculator uses:

ab+cd=ad+bcbd\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad+bc}{bd}

The result is then reduced to lowest terms.

How does the calculator subtract fractions?

It cross-multiplies the numerators and denominators using:

ab−cd=ad−bcbd\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad-bc}{bd}

The resulting fraction is simplified afterward.

How are fractions multiplied?

Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together:

ab×cd=acbd\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ac}{bd}

The final result is then reduced when possible.

How do you divide one fraction by another?

Division uses the reciprocal of the second fraction:

ab÷cd=ab×dc\frac{a}{b}\div\frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b}\times\frac{d}{c}

This gives:

adbc\frac{ad}{bc}

The second fraction cannot be zero.

How does the calculator simplify a fraction?

It finds the greatest common divisor of the numerator and denominator and divides both values by it.

For example, 20/30 has a greatest common divisor of 10, so:

2030=23\frac{20}{30}=\frac{2}{3}
Why can the decimal result have many digits?

Some fractions have repeating decimal expansions. For example, 1/3 equals 0.3333… indefinitely. A digital decimal display can show only a finite number of digits, while the fraction remains exact.

Can I enter extremely large integers?

The calculator uses standard JavaScript number handling. Extremely large integers can exceed JavaScript's exact integer precision, so arbitrary-precision tools are more suitable for very large-number fraction calculations.

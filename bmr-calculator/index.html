What Is a BMR Calculator?

Answer: A BMR calculator estimates resting energy expenditure from age, sex, weight, and height. The Mifflin–St Jeor equation was developed as a predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals.

How to Use the BMR Calculator

  1. Enter sex, age, body weight, and height.
  2. Calculate the predicted resting energy value.
  3. Review activity-multiplied estimates only as rough daily-energy comparisons.

Mifflin–St Jeor Equation

Men: REE = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5 Women: REE = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161

W is kilograms, H is centimeters, and A is age in years.

Calculation Example

For a 30-year-old, 70 kg, 175 cm male, the equation gives 10×70 + 6.25×175 − 5×30 + 5 ≈ 1,649 kcal/day.

Assumptions and Limitations

Predictive equations have individual error and do not replace indirect calorimetry. Illness, pregnancy, body composition, medications, environment, and other factors can affect actual energy expenditure.

BMR Calculator FAQs

Is BMR exactly how many calories I burn each day?

No. BMR or resting-energy estimates cover basic resting needs; total daily expenditure also includes activity and the thermic effect of food.

Why do men and women have different constants?

The Mifflin–St Jeor predictive equations use sex-specific constants derived from the study population.

Is this measured BMR?

No. This is a prediction from an equation, not a metabolic measurement.

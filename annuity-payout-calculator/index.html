What Is an Annuity Payout Calculator?

An annuity payout calculator estimates either the level payment supported by a starting balance over a fixed term or how long a balance may last when you choose a fixed payment. The model assumes a constant return and end-of-period withdrawals.

Fixed-Length Annuity Payout Formula

Payment = PV × i ÷ [1 − (1 + i)−n]

PV is the starting balance, i is the periodic return, and n is the number of payments. If the return rate is zero, payment equals principal divided by the number of payments.

Fixed-Payment Length Formula

n = −ln(1 − PV × i ÷ Payment) ÷ ln(1 + i)

This formula requires the payment to be greater than the interest generated per period if the balance is expected to decline to zero under a positive constant rate.

Understanding Your Results

Total payments include returned principal plus modeled investment return. The annual table shows the beginning balance, modeled return, and ending balance as withdrawals reduce the account.

Limitations

Actual investments fluctuate and commercial annuity payouts may include mortality credits, contract fees, guarantees, taxes, insurer strength, and surrender terms. This model does not represent those features.

Frequently Asked Questions

What payment formula does the fixed-length mode use?

For a level end-of-period withdrawal, the calculator uses Payment = PV × i ÷ [1 − (1 + i)^−n], where i is the periodic rate and n is the number of payouts.

When can a fixed payment last forever mathematically?

If a fixed withdrawal is less than or equal to the interest generated each period, a constant-rate mathematical model may not amortize the balance to zero. Real returns are not guaranteed, so this is not a perpetual-income promise.

Does this include annuity insurer guarantees?

No. This calculator models a balance with constant return and level withdrawals. Commercial annuity contracts can have guarantees, mortality credits, expenses, surrender terms, and insurer-specific rules.

What Is a Square Footage Calculator?

A square footage calculator finds the area of a floor, wall, lot section, or other surface. This version supports rectangles, triangles, and circles, converts the result to square meters and square yards, and can multiply the area by a price per square foot.

Square Footage Formulas

Rectangle: A = length × width
Triangle: A = base × height ÷ 2
Circle: A = π × (diameter ÷ 2)²

Convert dimensions to the same linear unit before applying an area formula. The calculator converts input feet, inches, or meters to feet internally, then reports square feet.

Example

A rectangular room that is 12 ft long and 10 ft wide has an area of 120 ft². Using the exact conversion 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m², that is about 11.15 m².

How to Measure Complex Areas

For an irregular room or lot, divide the outline into simple shapes. Calculate each section separately and add the areas. ENERGY STAR uses the same general approach when explaining room measurements for air-conditioner sizing.

Limitations

Real construction projects usually need a waste allowance for cuts, breakage, pattern matching, and offcuts. This calculator gives geometric area only unless you intentionally increase the quantity or material order.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate square footage of a rectangle?

Multiply length by width after converting both measurements to feet. The result is square feet.

How do I calculate square footage of a triangle?

Multiply base by height and divide by two. Use perpendicular height, not a sloped side.

Can I use this for complex rooms?

Yes. Split the space into simple rectangles, triangles, or circles, calculate each area, and add the results.

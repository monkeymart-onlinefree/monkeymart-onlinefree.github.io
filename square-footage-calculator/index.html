Use the Square Footage Calculator to find the area of rectangular, triangular, or circular spaces. Enter the required dimensions, quantity, and optional price per square foot to calculate square footage and supporting area or cost values.

What Is the Square Footage Calculator?

The Square Footage Calculator measures the two-dimensional area of common shapes in square feet. It can be used for rooms, floors, walls, yards, patios, flooring projects, and other spaces where you need an area measurement.

The calculator supports rectangle, triangle, and circle area formulas. Measurements entered in feet, inches, or meters are converted to feet internally before the area calculation is performed.

How to Use the Square Footage Calculator

Enter the measurements requested for the area you want to calculate.

Length: Enter the length or first required dimension of the area. Use the measurement unit that matches your value.

Width: Enter the width or second required dimension when the selected shape needs two dimensions.

Quantity: Enter the number of matching areas or sections you want included. Use 1 when calculating a single area.

Price per ft²: This optional input can be used when you want a cost estimate based on the calculated square footage. Leave it empty when you only need the area.

Make sure each measurement uses the correct unit. The calculator normalizes supported measurements to feet before calculating the area.

Formula / Calculation Method

The calculator first converts the entered dimensions to the same linear unit—feet—and then applies the appropriate geometry formula. Standard references give the rectangle area as length × width, triangle area as one-half base × perpendicular height, and circle area as π × radius².

Rectangle Formula

For a rectangular area:

A = L × W

Where:

Symbol Meaning Unit
A Area ft²
L Length ft
W Width ft

For example, a rectangle 12 feet long and 10 feet wide has an area of:

12 × 10 = 120 ft²

Triangle Formula

For a triangle:

A = (b × h) ÷ 2

Where:

Symbol Meaning Unit
A Area ft²
b Base ft
h Perpendicular height ft

The height must be measured perpendicular to the base.

Circle Formula

For a circle:

A = π × (d ÷ 2)²

Because the radius is half the diameter:

r = d ÷ 2

So the equivalent standard formula is:

A = πr²

Where:

Symbol Meaning Unit
A Area ft²
d Diameter ft
r Radius ft
π Pi, approximately 3.14159 No unit

Unit Conversion

The calculator converts supported linear measurements to feet before applying the area formula.

For inches:

Feet = Inches ÷ 12

For meters:

Feet = Meters ÷ 0.3048

After calculating square footage, the square-meter equivalent can be found using:

Square meters = Square feet × 0.09290304

NIST lists 1 square foot as exactly 929.0304 square centimeters, equivalent to 0.09290304 m².

Calculation Sequence

  1. Read the entered dimensions and their measurement units.
  2. Convert the dimensions to feet when necessary.
  3. Apply the formula for the selected geometric shape.
  4. Report the calculated area in square feet.
  5. Convert the area to square meters for the supporting metric result.
  6. Use the quantity and optional price-per-square-foot inputs when applicable to the calculator's supporting results.

Worked Example

Suppose you want to calculate the square footage of one rectangular room that is 12 feet long and 10 feet wide.

Inputs

  • Length: 12 ft
  • Width: 10 ft
  • Quantity: 1
  • Price per ft²: Not entered

Step 1: Apply the Rectangle Formula

A = L × W

Substitute the measurements:

A = 12 × 10

Step 2: Calculate Square Feet

A = 120 ft²

Step 3: Convert to Square Meters

120 × 0.09290304 = 11.1483648 m²

Final Result

The room has an area of:

120 square feet

or approximately:

11.15 square meters

The square-meter figure is rounded here for readability. The calculator's displayed precision may depend on its result formatting.

Understanding Your Results

Square Footage

Square footage is the main area result. It represents how much two-dimensional surface the measured shape covers in square feet.

Square Meters

The square-meter result expresses the same area using the metric system. This is useful when measurements, plans, products, or project requirements use metric units.

Quantity

Quantity is useful when several areas have the same dimensions. For a single room, panel, section, or surface, use a quantity of 1.

Estimated Cost

When a price per square foot is entered, the calculator can provide supporting cost information based on the calculated area. Treat this as a planning estimate rather than a final supplier, contractor, or construction quote.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator uses standard geometric area formulas. Its result depends directly on the measurements and units you enter, so incorrect measurements or unit selections will produce incorrect area results.

Measurements in feet, inches, or meters are normalized to feet before the square-foot calculation. Small differences may appear when displayed results are rounded.

The calculator determines geometric area. It does not automatically add a construction waste allowance for cutting, breakage, offcuts, pattern matching, installation losses, or damaged material. Real flooring, tile, roofing, and similar projects may therefore require more material than the geometric area alone.

For irregular spaces, separate the space into simpler measurable shapes where practical and calculate the required sections individually.

Optional cost results should be treated as estimates. Actual project prices can also include labor, taxes, delivery, preparation, accessories, minimum order quantities, and other costs that are outside a basic square-footage calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the square footage of a rectangle?

Convert both dimensions to feet and multiply the length by the width. A 15 ft × 12 ft rectangle has an area of 180 ft².

Can I use inches or meters?

Yes. The calculation method supports feet, inches, and meters by converting the dimensions to feet before applying the area formula.

How is the square footage of a circle calculated?

The calculator uses the diameter to find the radius and then applies A = π × (d ÷ 2)². A radius is always half the circle's diameter.

How do I calculate the area of a triangle?

Multiply the base by the perpendicular height and divide the result by 2:

A = (b × h) ÷ 2

The height should be perpendicular to the selected base.

Does the calculator automatically include extra material for waste?

No. The geometric area does not automatically include waste for cuts, damaged material, pattern alignment, breakage, or offcuts. Add any required project allowance separately.

What does price per square foot mean?

Price per square foot is an optional project-pricing input. It allows the calculator to provide cost-related supporting information based on the calculated area when a price is supplied.

How accurate is a square footage calculation?

The formulas themselves are standard geometry formulas, but the practical accuracy depends on your measurements, selected units, shape approximation, and result rounding. Measure carefully when the result will be used to purchase materials.

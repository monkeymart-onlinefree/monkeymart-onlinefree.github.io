The Age Calculator finds the age or elapsed calendar time between a Date of Birth and an Age at Date. It shows the result in years, months, and days, along with total days, weeks, hours, and the next birthday.

What Is the Age Calculator?

The Age Calculator measures how much calendar time has passed between two dates. Instead of assuming every month has the same number of days, it counts complete calendar years, months, and remaining days.

You can use it to check your current age, find your age on a past or future date, calculate total elapsed days, or see when your next birthday occurs.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Date of Birth

Enter the starting date used for the age calculation. For a person's age, this is their birth date.

Age at Date

Enter the date on which you want the age calculated. Use today's date for a current-age calculation, or enter another date to calculate age at that point in time.

After both dates are entered, the calculator returns the calendar age and supporting elapsed-time results.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

Age is calculated using calendar years, calendar months, and calendar days rather than converting the complete interval into a fixed number of 365-day years or 30-day months.

The result can be expressed as:

Calendar Age = Y years + M months + D days

where the components represent complete calendar units between the two entered dates.

This approach is important because months have different lengths. Month-end calculations can also have more than one reasonable representation; this method uses the approach in which days are borrowed from the preceding target month when required. Similar month-end ambiguity is documented for age calculations using dates such as February 28 through March 31.

Variables

Symbol Meaning Unit
B Date of Birth Calendar date
A Age at Date Calendar date
Y Complete years Years
M Remaining complete months Months
D Remaining calendar days Days
E Total elapsed days between B and A Days

Calculation Sequence

  1. Start with the Date of Birth and Age at Date.
  2. Count the complete calendar years between the two dates.
  3. Count the remaining complete calendar months.
  4. Count the remaining calendar days.
  5. If the day component requires adjustment, days are borrowed from the preceding target month.
  6. Calculate the total elapsed days separately from the years-months-days result.
  7. Express the elapsed-day total in weeks and hours.
  8. Identify the next occurrence of the birth month and day after the selected Age at Date.

Because this is calendar arithmetic, the years-months-days result should not be recreated by simply dividing total days by 365 or months by 30.

Total Days

The calculator separately measures the elapsed calendar days between the two entered dates:

Total Days = Age at Date − Date of Birth

This value is independent of the displayed years, months, and days breakdown.

Total Weeks

The elapsed-day total can be expressed in weeks:

Total Weeks = Total Days ÷ 7

A fractional result represents additional days beyond the completed weeks.

Total Hours

For the calculator's date-based elapsed-time conversion:

Total Hours = Total Days × 24

Because the inputs contain dates rather than exact birth times, this should be understood as a date-based hour total rather than a timestamp-level age from the exact hour and minute of birth.

Next Birthday

The calculator identifies the next calendar occurrence of the birth month and day relative to the selected Age at Date. The remaining interval to that date provides the time until the next birthday.

Worked Example

Suppose the following dates are entered.

Inputs

  • Date of Birth: January 15, 2000
  • Age at Date: June 20, 2025

Step 1: Count Complete Years

From January 15, 2000 to January 15, 2025:

25 complete years

Step 2: Count Complete Months

From January 15, 2025 to June 15, 2025:

5 complete months

Step 3: Count Remaining Days

From June 15 to June 20:

5 days

Therefore:

Calendar Age = 25 years, 5 months, 5 days

The elapsed-day calculation is performed separately:

Total Days = 9,288 days

Then:

Total Weeks = 9,288 ÷ 7 = 1,326.857... weeks

Total Hours = 9,288 × 24 = 222,912 hours

The next birthday is:

January 15, 2026

From June 20, 2025, that birthday is 209 days away.

Final Result

For this example, the calculated calendar age is:

25 years, 5 months, 5 days

Supporting results are 9,288 total days, approximately 1,326.86 weeks, and 222,912 hours.

Understanding Your Results

Calendar Age

Calendar Age is the main result. It separates the interval into complete years, complete months, and remaining days while respecting the actual lengths of calendar months.

Total Days

Total Days shows the complete date-to-date interval as days. It is useful when you need one continuous unit instead of a years-months-days breakdown.

Total Weeks

Total Weeks expresses the same elapsed period in seven-day units. It may include a decimal because the number of elapsed days may not divide evenly by seven.

Total Hours

Total Hours converts the date-based elapsed-day total into hours. Since the calculator does not request a birth time or target time, it is not intended to measure age from an exact time-of-day timestamp.

Next Birthday

Next Birthday shows the upcoming calendar birthday after the selected Age at Date, helping you see when the next yearly birthday occurs.

Assumptions and Limitations

Calendar months do not have equal lengths, so age cannot always be represented uniquely by using simple fixed-length month conversions. Month-end dates are especially important: depending on the convention used, the same pair of dates can sometimes produce more than one reasonable months-and-days representation. This calculator uses a method that borrows days from the preceding target month when required.

The calendar age and total elapsed days are separate calculations. For example, a calendar age of one year does not always equal exactly 365 days because calendar years can have different numbers of days.

The calculator accepts dates rather than exact times. Its hour result therefore represents the date-based elapsed interval and should not be treated as the exact number of hours since a person's recorded time of birth.

Browser date functions can depend on the host environment's calendar and local time-zone rules. ECMAScript's time-zone-aware date handling uses local political time rules and is designed to work with IANA Time Zone Database information.

Current Browser and Time-Zone Data

This calculator does not require a changing interest rate, legal threshold, or yearly financial limit. However, browser time-zone data can change when governments alter local clock rules.

As of September 24, 2026, the latest IANA Time Zone Database release identified during verification is 2026b, released on April 22, 2026.

For ordinary date-of-birth calculations, the main result remains based on calendar dates rather than a user-selected named time zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Age Calculator calculate age?

It counts complete calendar years, then complete calendar months, and then the remaining days between the Date of Birth and Age at Date. Total elapsed days are calculated separately.

Why not divide the number of days by 365?

A fixed 365-day division does not reproduce calendar age correctly in every case because calendar years and months have different lengths. Calendar age preserves actual year, month, and day boundaries.

Why can month-end age calculations be confusing?

Months do not all end on the same day number. For example, February can end on the 28th or 29th, while March ends on the 31st. Different valid conventions can therefore describe some month-end intervals differently.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Calendar-date arithmetic uses the actual dates in the calendar rather than treating every year as exactly 365 days. Leap days can therefore affect the total elapsed-day result.

What is the difference between calendar age and total days?

Calendar age divides the interval into years, months, and days. Total Days expresses the entire interval as one continuous number of elapsed days.

Does Total Hours use my exact birth time?

No. The available input is a Date of Birth, not a birth date and time. Total Hours therefore represents the date-based elapsed period rather than the exact time since a recorded birth timestamp.

Can I calculate my age on another date?

Yes. Enter your Date of Birth and use the required past or future date in the Age at Date field. The result will represent the age between those two dates.

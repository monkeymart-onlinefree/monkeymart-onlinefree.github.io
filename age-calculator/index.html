What Is an Age Calculator?

Answer: An age calculator finds the calendar interval between a date of birth and another date. In the common age system, age increases on the birthday.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Choose the date at which age should be calculated.
  3. Calculate the calendar age.
  4. Review total days and the next birthday.

Calendar Age Method

Years, months, and days are counted as calendar units rather than treating every month as the same number of days. Total elapsed days are calculated separately from the date difference.

Calculation Example

A person born January 15, 2000 is 26 years old on January 15, 2026; before that birthday in 2026, their completed age is 25.

Assumptions and Limitations

Month-end dates can be represented in more than one reasonable way because calendar months have different lengths. This implementation borrows days from the preceding target month when needed.

Age Calculator FAQs

Does age increase on the birthday?

Yes, in the common age system used by this calculator.

Can I calculate age at a past date?

Yes, as long as the target date is not before the birth date.

Why can month/day intervals be tricky?

Months have unequal lengths, so calendar interval calculations need a defined borrowing convention.

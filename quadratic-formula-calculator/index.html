What Is a Quadratic Formula Calculator?

This calculator solves equations in the form ax² + bx + c = 0 where a ≠ 0. It returns real or complex roots, the discriminant, the root classification, the parabola’s vertex, and its vertical axis of symmetry.

How to Use the Quadratic Formula Calculator

  1. Enter coefficients a, b, and c.
  2. Make sure a is not zero.
  3. Select Solve Equation to calculate the roots and supporting values.

Quadratic Formula

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / (2a)

The quantity b² − 4ac is called the discriminant. Its sign determines whether the roots are two distinct real numbers, one repeated real number, or a complex-conjugate pair.

Calculation Example

For x² − 3x + 2 = 0, the discriminant is 1. The two roots are x = 2 and x = 1.

Limitations

The quadratic formula applies only when the coefficient a is nonzero. Results use floating-point arithmetic, so irrational and complex values are displayed as rounded decimal approximations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when the discriminant is zero?

The equation has one repeated real root at x = −b/(2a). Geometrically, the parabola touches the x-axis at its vertex.

Why are there two signs in ±?

The plus and minus produce the two possible roots of the quadratic equation. They are identical only when the discriminant equals zero.

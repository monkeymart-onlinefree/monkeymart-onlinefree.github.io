What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator finds the final price and amount saved after a percentage discount or fixed-amount reduction. It can also apply a second stackable percentage discount to the already-discounted price.

Discount Formulas

For percentage discounts: final = original × (1 − discount rate). For a fixed discount: final = original − fixed amount. A second percentage discount is multiplied against the first discounted price, not simply added to the first percentage.

Example

A $279 item at 20% off costs $223.20 after the first discount. Another 15% off that reduced price gives $189.72. The effective combined discount is 32%, not 35%.

Why Stackable Discounts Are Not Added

Each discount applies to a different base. After the first reduction, the second percentage is applied to a smaller number. Combined effect is found by multiplying the remaining-price factors.

Limitations

This calculator does not automatically add sales tax, shipping, coupon caps, minimum-spend rules, exclusions, membership pricing, or quantity discounts. Retail terms determine the real final checkout price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I combine 20% off and 15% off?

Apply them sequentially: multiply the price by 0.80, then multiply that result by 0.85. The combined remaining-price factor is 0.68, so the effective discount is 32%.

Can a fixed discount make the price negative?

No. This calculator floors the discounted price at zero. Real promotions may also have minimum-purchase rules or exclusions.

Does the calculator include sales tax?

No. It calculates the discount before any separate sales-tax calculation. Tax rules and whether tax is based on pre- or post-discount price depend on the jurisdiction and promotion.

What Is a Bra Size Calculator?

A bra size calculator uses an underbust measurement to estimate a band and the difference between full bust and band to estimate a cup. The result is a starting point for fitting, not a universal guarantee, because brands and sizing systems differ.

How to Measure

Measure the underbust snugly around the ribcage directly beneath the bust. Measure the full bust around the fullest part without compressing tissue. Keep the tape level and use the same unit for both measurements.

How Cup Size Is Estimated

A common starting chart treats roughly 1 inch of bust-to-band difference as A, 2 inches as B, 3 inches as C, and 4 inches as D. Cup labels above D diverge between U.S., U.K., and European systems.

Limitations and Fit Check

Bra sizing is not globally standardized. A good fit also depends on cup shape, wire width, fabric stretch, and brand construction. The band should sit level, and the cups should contain tissue without obvious spilling or gaping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a calculated bra size guaranteed to fit?

No. It is a measurement-based starting point. Brand patterns, cup shape, fabric, and personal comfort can change the best fit.

Why do US and UK cup labels differ?

The cup-letter sequences diverge above D, so the same body measurements can have different labels across sizing systems.

Should I add four inches to my underbust?

Sizing methods vary. This calculator uses a direct-band starting method rather than automatically adding four inches; always compare with the brand's own chart.

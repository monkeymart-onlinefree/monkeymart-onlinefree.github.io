How it works

This calculator models a loan with no periodic payments: interest compounds into the balance and the full amount is due at the end of the selected term.

Maturity balance = Principal × (1 + annual rate ÷ compounding periods)^(compounding periods × years)

For a partial final year, the final balance uses the matching fractional compounding period. The schedule shows annual checkpoints.

Example

A $100,000 loan at 6% compounded annually for 10 years grows to $179,084.77, including $79,084.77 in accrued interest. This is a mathematical estimate, not a lender payoff quote.

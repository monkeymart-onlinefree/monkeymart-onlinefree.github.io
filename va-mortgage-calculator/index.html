Use the VA Mortgage Calculator to estimate the monthly cost of a VA-backed purchase loan. Enter the home price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, VA benefit use, property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, and funding-fee status to estimate the financed loan amount and monthly housing cost.

What Is the VA Mortgage Calculator?

The VA Mortgage Calculator estimates the cost of financing a home with a VA-backed purchase loan. It calculates the base loan after your down payment, determines the applicable VA funding fee, optionally adds that fee to the financed balance, and calculates monthly principal and interest.

It also adds estimated property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues so you can see a broader monthly housing-cost estimate.

The calculator is useful for comparing different home prices, down payments, interest rates, loan terms, and VA funding-fee scenarios before requesting a lender-specific Loan Estimate.

How to Use the VA Mortgage Calculator

Home Price

Enter the purchase price of the home in dollars. This amount is used to calculate the down payment, base loan, property-tax estimate, and other loan values.

Down Payment (%)

Enter the down payment as a percentage of the home price.

For example, a 5% down payment on a $400,000 home equals:

$400,000 × 5% = $20,000

The entered percentage can also affect the VA funding-fee rate.

Interest Rate (%)

Enter the annual mortgage interest rate you want to use for the estimate. The calculator converts this annual percentage into a monthly rate for the mortgage-payment calculation.

The interest rate is supplied by you; the calculator does not retrieve or predict a current lender rate.

Term (Years)

Enter the mortgage term in years. The calculator converts the term into monthly payment periods.

For example:

30 years × 12 = 360 monthly payments

VA Benefit Use

Select whether this is your:

  • First use of the VA home-loan benefit
  • Use after your first VA-backed or VA direct home loan

This selection matters because the funding fee for a purchase loan with less than 5% down is higher for subsequent use.

Property Tax (%/Year)

Enter your estimated annual property-tax rate as a percentage of the home's price.

Property taxes vary by location and may change over time, so use an estimate appropriate for the property.

Home Insurance ($/Year)

Enter the estimated annual homeowners-insurance premium in dollars. The calculator divides this amount by 12 to estimate the monthly insurance cost.

HOA ($/Month)

Enter any monthly homeowners-association fee. Enter 0 if no HOA fee applies.

Funding-Fee Exempt

Select the exemption option if you qualify for an official VA funding-fee exemption. When the borrower is exempt, the funding fee used in the calculation is $0.

Finance Funding Fee

Choose whether the VA funding fee will be included in the financed loan balance.

If financed, the funding fee increases the principal used to calculate monthly principal and interest. If it is not financed, the base mortgage amount is used for the monthly payment and the funding fee is treated separately.

Formula / Calculation Method

1. Down Payment Amount

Let:

  • H = home price
  • d = down payment percentage

The dollar down payment is:

D = H × (d ÷ 100)

2. Base Loan Amount

The mortgage amount before any financed VA funding fee is:

B = H − D

Where:

  • B = base loan amount
  • H = home price
  • D = down payment amount

3. VA Funding Fee

If the borrower is not exempt, the calculator selects a funding-fee percentage based on the down payment and whether this is the first or a subsequent use of the VA benefit.

The fee is calculated from the base loan amount:

F = B × f

Where:

  • F = VA funding fee
  • B = base loan amount
  • f = applicable VA funding-fee rate as a decimal

If the borrower is funding-fee exempt:

F = $0

VA states that the funding fee for a purchase loan applies to the loan amount rather than the home's full purchase price.

4. Financed Loan Amount

If the funding fee is financed:

P = B + F

If the funding fee is paid separately:

P = B

Where P is the principal used in the monthly principal-and-interest calculation.

VA allows the funding fee to be paid at closing or included in the loan. For a VA purchase or construction loan, other closing costs generally cannot be financed into the loan in the same way.

5. Monthly Principal and Interest

The calculator uses the standard fixed-payment mortgage formula:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • M = monthly principal and interest
  • P = financed principal
  • r = monthly interest rate
  • n = total number of monthly payments

The monthly interest rate is:

r = (Annual Interest Rate ÷ 100) ÷ 12

The number of payments is:

n = Term in Years × 12

A fixed-rate mortgage payment depends on the principal, interest rate, and loan term. Taxes and homeowners insurance are separate components that can make the total monthly housing payment higher than principal and interest alone.

6. Monthly Property Tax

Let t be the annual property-tax percentage.

Monthly Property Tax = H × (t ÷ 100) ÷ 12

7. Monthly Home Insurance

Monthly Insurance = Annual Home Insurance ÷ 12

8. Monthly HOA Cost

The entered monthly HOA amount is added directly to the housing-cost estimate.

9. Estimated Total Monthly Cost

The calculator combines:

Estimated Monthly Cost = Principal & Interest + Property Tax + Home Insurance + HOA

HOA charges may be paid separately from the mortgage servicer even though they are included here when estimating overall monthly housing costs.

Worked Example

Assume the following VA purchase-loan scenario.

Input Example
Home price$400,000
Down payment5%
Interest rate6.50%
Term30 years
VA benefit useFirst use
Property tax1.20% per year

Step 1: Calculate the Down Payment

$400,000 × 5% = $20,000

Step 2: Calculate the Base Loan

$400,000 − $20,000 = $380,000

Step 3: Calculate the VA Funding Fee

For a first-use VA purchase loan with at least 5% but less than 10% down, the current funding-fee rate is 1.50%.

$380,000 × 1.50% = $5,700

Step 4: Calculate the Financed Loan Amount

Because the example finances the funding fee:

$380,000 + $5,700 = $385,700

Step 5: Calculate Monthly Principal and Interest

Monthly interest rate:

r = 0.065 ÷ 12 = 0.0054166667

Number of monthly payments:

n = 30 × 12 = 360

Using:

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

gives estimated monthly principal and interest of:

$2,437.89

Step 6: Calculate Monthly Property Tax

Annual tax:

$400,000 × 1.20% = $4,800

Monthly tax:

$4,800 ÷ 12 = $400

Step 7: Calculate Monthly Insurance

$1,800 ÷ 12 = $150

Final Result

Estimated monthly housing cost:

$2,437.89 + $400 + $150 + $100 = $3,087.89 per month

This example does not add transaction-specific closing costs, maintenance, utilities, or other expenses not included in the calculator.

Understanding Your Results

Base Loan Amount

This is the home's price minus the down payment. It is the loan amount before adding a financed VA funding fee.

VA Funding Fee

This is the one-time VA fee calculated from the applicable funding-fee percentage and base loan amount. It becomes $0 when the borrower qualifies for an exemption.

Financed Loan Amount

This is the principal used for the mortgage calculation when the funding fee is financed.

Financing the fee increases the amount on which mortgage interest is calculated.

Monthly Principal and Interest

This is the estimated monthly payment needed to amortize the financed principal over the selected term at the entered interest rate.

It does not by itself include property taxes, insurance, or HOA dues.

Property Tax and Insurance

These amounts estimate recurring housing expenses in monthly terms. Actual tax and insurance amounts can differ from estimates and can change after the loan begins.

Estimated Monthly Cost

This combines the calculator's estimated principal and interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues.

It should be treated as a planning estimate rather than a lender quote. Your lender's Loan Estimate provides transaction-specific loan and closing-cost information.

Current VA Funding-Fee Rules

Current data checked: September 24, 2026.

VA's current purchase-loan funding-fee rates are effective for loans closing on or after April 7, 2023. VA Circular 26-23-6 states that this rate schedule applies to loans closing before November 14, 2031.

Down Payment First Use After First Use
Less than 5% 2.15% 3.30%
5% or more, but less than 10% 1.50% 1.50%
10% or more 1.25% 1.25%

The 5% funding-fee threshold is based on the actual down payment percentage; VA guidance specifies that the percentage should not simply be rounded up to reach the threshold.

Funding-Fee Exemptions

VA currently states that the funding fee may not be required when qualifying conditions apply, including certain borrowers receiving or eligible for service-connected disability compensation, qualifying surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, certain service members with a pre-discharge disability rating, and active-duty Purple Heart recipients who provide the required evidence by closing.

Use the calculator's Funding-Fee Exempt option only when the exemption applies to your situation.

VA Loan Entitlement and Limits

VA states that borrowers with full entitlement do not have a VA home-loan limit, although the lender must still approve the loan based on factors such as income, debts, assets, credit, and the property's appraised value. Borrowers without full entitlement can be affected by county loan limits and remaining entitlement. This calculator does not calculate VA entitlement.

Assumptions and Limitations

The VA Mortgage Calculator provides an estimate based on the values you enter. It does not determine whether you qualify for a VA-backed mortgage.

The calculation assumes a fixed interest rate over the selected loan term and uses the standard amortizing-payment formula.

Funding-fee calculations depend on the selected benefit-use status, down payment, exemption status, and current VA fee schedule. A lender or VA determination should be used to confirm the actual fee.

Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues are estimates. These costs may change and may be billed or collected differently from the monthly amounts shown.

The calculator does not determine VA eligibility, Certificate of Eligibility status, remaining entitlement, residual-income requirements, lender underwriting, property eligibility, appraisal results, credit requirements, closing-cost eligibility, or interest-rate qualification.

It also does not calculate every possible transaction expense. Use your lender's official Loan Estimate to review your specific loan amount, payment, projected taxes and insurance, closing costs, and cash required at closing.

Small differences can also occur when lenders or other calculators use different rounding conventions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the VA funding fee?

The VA funding fee is a one-time fee that generally applies to VA-backed and VA direct home loans unless an exemption applies. For purchase loans, the percentage depends on factors including the down payment and whether the VA home-loan benefit has been used before.

Is the VA funding fee calculated from the home price?

For a purchase loan, the fee is applied to the loan amount after the down payment rather than to the full purchase price. For example, if a $400,000 home has a $20,000 down payment, the applicable fee is calculated from the $380,000 base loan.

Can I finance the VA funding fee?

Yes. VA allows the funding fee to be included in the loan or paid in full at closing. Financing it increases the mortgage principal and therefore increases the principal-and-interest payment.

Does a larger down payment reduce the VA funding fee?

It can. Under the current purchase-loan schedule, non-exempt borrowers receive lower rates at down payments of at least 5% and at least 10%.

Why doesn't this calculator include monthly PMI?

VA states that its home-loan program does not require monthly private mortgage insurance. A VA funding fee may apply instead unless the borrower qualifies for an exemption.

Does the calculator tell me whether I qualify for a VA loan?

No. Eligibility and entitlement are separate from this payment estimate. Lenders also evaluate financial qualifications, and VA eligibility generally involves a Certificate of Eligibility.

Why might my lender's monthly payment be different?

Your actual payment can differ because of the final interest rate, confirmed funding-fee status, property taxes, insurance premiums, escrow requirements, loan amount, lender calculations, and other transaction-specific details. Compare the estimate with the Loan Estimate issued by your lender.

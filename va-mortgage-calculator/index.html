What Is a VA Mortgage Calculator?

A VA mortgage calculator estimates payment and loan costs for a VA-backed home purchase. It can account for the VA funding fee, down payment, loan term, interest rate, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues.

VA Funding Fee Rates Used

VA’s current purchase-loan funding fee table is effective April 7, 2023. For first use, the fee is 2.15% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with at least 5% down, and 1.25% with at least 10% down. For subsequent use, the under-5% tier is 3.3%, while the 5% and 10% tiers remain 1.5% and 1.25%.

Funding-Fee Exemptions

VA lists several funding-fee exemptions, including qualifying borrowers receiving or eligible for certain service-connected disability compensation. This calculator lets you mark the scenario as exempt, but it does not determine exemption status.

No Monthly VA Mortgage Insurance

VA explains that its home-loan program does not require a down payment or monthly mortgage insurance in the same way many other low-down-payment loan programs do, though lender and borrower qualification rules still apply.

Limitations

This tool does not determine VA eligibility, entitlement, residual-income requirements, lender underwriting, property eligibility, closing-cost rules, or interest-rate qualification. Use your lender’s Loan Estimate for transaction-specific costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a VA home loan require monthly mortgage insurance?

VA states that its home-loan program does not require monthly mortgage insurance. A one-time VA funding fee may apply unless the borrower is exempt.

Can the VA funding fee be financed?

VA says the funding fee can generally be financed into the loan or paid in full at closing. Other purchase closing costs generally cannot all be financed into the VA loan.

What affects the VA funding fee?

For purchase loans, the funding fee depends on down payment size and whether it is the first or a subsequent use of the VA home-loan benefit. Exempt borrowers pay no funding fee.

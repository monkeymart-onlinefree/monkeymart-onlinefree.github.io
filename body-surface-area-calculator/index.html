What Is a Body Surface Area Calculator?

A BSA calculator estimates total body surface area from height and weight. BSA is used in several clinical contexts, including indexing physiologic measurements and some medication-dosing protocols.

How to Use the BSA Calculator

Choose your unit system, enter height and weight, and select sex if you want the Schlich formula to be calculated. The page shows Mosteller as the primary result plus several other established formulas for comparison.

Mosteller Formula

BSA (m²) = √[(height cm × weight kg) / 3600]. This is algebraically equivalent to 0.016667 × W^0.5 × H^0.5.

Other Formulas

The comparison panel includes Du Bois, Haycock, Gehan & George, and Schlich estimates. Differences are usually modest for average body sizes but can become more noticeable at extremes.

Limitations

BSA is an estimate. Do not use an online BSA result alone to select chemotherapy or other medication doses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is body surface area?

Body surface area (BSA) is an estimate of the external surface area of the body, usually expressed in square meters.

Which BSA formula is shown as the main result?

The main result uses the Mosteller formula: square root of height in centimeters times weight in kilograms divided by 3600.

Why do different BSA formulas give different answers?

The formulas were developed from different datasets and mathematical fits, so they are close but not identical.

Can I use this result to dose medication myself?

No. Medication dosing can require clinical judgment, drug-specific rules, organ-function assessment, and professional verification.

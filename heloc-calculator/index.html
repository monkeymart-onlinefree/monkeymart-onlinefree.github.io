Use this HELOC Calculator to estimate monthly draw-period interest and a monthly repayment-period payment from your HELOC balance, APR, and repayment term. It also provides supporting estimates for HELOC utilization, combined loan-to-value (CLTV), and available home equity.

What Is the HELOC Calculator?

A home equity line of credit, or HELOC, is a revolving line of credit secured by your home equity. During the draw period, you can generally borrow up to the available credit limit; after the draw period, the account normally enters a repayment period. HELOC terms and payment structures vary by lender, and HELOC rates are commonly variable.

This calculator is useful for estimating the interest cost on your current HELOC balance and the payment required if that balance is amortized over your selected repayment term. It can also help you review your home-equity position using your mortgage balance, HELOC information, home value, and planning CLTV percentage.

How to Use the HELOC Calculator

Enter the following information:

  • Home value: Your estimated current property value.
  • Existing mortgage: The outstanding balance on your existing mortgage.
  • HELOC credit limit: The maximum credit line available under your HELOC.
  • Balance drawn: The HELOC amount currently outstanding.
  • Current APR (%): The annual percentage rate used by the calculator for the interest and repayment estimates.
  • Repayment term (years): The number of years used to amortize the balance during repayment.
  • Planning max CLTV (%): Your chosen CLTV percentage for estimating your home-equity position. This is a planning input, not a universal lender approval limit.

Use values that match your actual HELOC or planning scenario. No universal minimum or maximum input ranges should be assumed because lender requirements vary.

Formula / Calculation Method

Draw-Period Interest Formula

The calculator estimates one month of interest on the outstanding HELOC balance using:

Monthly Interest = Outstanding Balance × Annual Rate ÷ 12

Because APR is entered as a percentage, it is first converted to decimal form:

Annual Rate = APR ÷ 100

Therefore:

Monthly Interest = Balance × (APR ÷ 100) ÷ 12

This is an interest-only estimate for one month. It does not automatically represent the minimum payment required by a particular lender.

Repayment-Period Payment Formula

The repayment estimate uses the standard amortizing payment formula:

M = P × r(1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]

Variables

  • M = estimated monthly repayment payment, in dollars
  • P = balance entering the repayment period, in dollars
  • r = monthly interest rate
  • n = total number of monthly repayment payments

The monthly rate is:

r = (APR ÷ 100) ÷ 12

The number of payments is:

n = Repayment Term in Years × 12

The formula produces a level monthly principal-and-interest payment when the balance, rate, and repayment term remain unchanged. Standard amortizing loans use payments designed to repay principal and interest over the selected term.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Convert the entered APR from a percentage to a decimal annual rate.
  2. Divide the annual rate by 12 to obtain the monthly rate.
  3. Calculate one month's interest on the outstanding balance.
  4. Convert the repayment term from years to months.
  5. Apply the amortizing payment formula to the balance entering repayment.
  6. Display the calculated payment and supporting HELOC/equity values.

Supporting HELOC Metrics

The calculator also reports utilization, CLTV, and available home equity as supporting values.

The documented calculation method for this page explicitly specifies the draw-period interest and repayment-payment equations but does not separately specify the exact equations used for these three supporting metrics. For that reason, no substitute or reconstructed formula is published here.

Worked Example

Suppose you enter:

Inputs

  • Home value: $500,000
  • Existing mortgage: $250,000
  • HELOC credit limit: $100,000
  • Balance drawn: $40,000
  • Current APR: 8.50%
  • Repayment term: 15 years
  • Planning max CLTV: 80%

Step 1: Convert the APR

Annual rate = 8.50 ÷ 100 = 0.085
Monthly rate = 0.085 ÷ 12 = 0.0070833333

Step 2: Calculate Monthly Draw-Period Interest

Monthly Interest = $40,000 × 0.085 ÷ 12
Monthly Interest = $283.33

The estimated interest-only cost for one month is therefore $283.33 if the $40,000 balance and 8.50% annual rate remain unchanged.

Step 3: Calculate the Repayment Payment

Repayment period:

15 × 12 = 180 monthly payments

Formula:

M = 40,000 × 0.0070833333 × (1.0070833333)¹⁸⁰ ÷ [(1.0070833333)¹⁸⁰ − 1]

Using full precision during the calculation:

M ≈ $393.90 per month

Final Result

For this example:

  • Estimated monthly draw-period interest: $283.33
  • Estimated repayment-period payment: $393.90 per month

The calculator may also display utilization, CLTV, and available-equity supporting values based on the other entered information.

Understanding Your Results

Draw-Period Interest

This is the estimated dollar interest cost for one month on the outstanding HELOC balance at the entered APR. It does not include principal repayment or lender-specific fees.

Repayment Payment

This is the estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment needed to amortize the balance entering repayment over the selected repayment term at the entered rate.

HELOC Utilization

Utilization is shown as a percentage and helps describe how much of the HELOC credit line is currently being used.

CLTV

CLTV is a percentage used to summarize debt secured by a property relative to its value. Your lender may use its own property valuation, balances, underwriting definitions, and lending limits.

Available Home Equity

This dollar estimate helps show remaining equity or borrowing room based on the values and planning assumptions entered into the calculator. It should not be treated as a loan approval amount.

Assumptions and Limitations

This calculator is a planning tool, not a lender quote or approval decision.

The draw-period calculation estimates monthly interest as the outstanding balance multiplied by the entered annual rate and divided by 12. It does not model daily interest accrual or lender-specific billing methods.

The repayment calculation assumes the rate used in the formula remains unchanged during the selected repayment term. In practice, HELOCs commonly have variable rates, so actual payments may change.

The calculator does not model future draws, additional principal payments, introductory rates, rate caps or floors, index-and-margin changes, closing costs, annual fees, transaction fees, minimum draw requirements, or other lender-specific charges unless they are already reflected in an entered value.

A lender's required minimum payment can differ from this calculator's interest-only estimate. Federal HELOC disclosure rules require creditors to explain how minimum periodic payments are determined, and repayment structures can vary between plans.

The Planning max CLTV field is a user-entered planning assumption. The calculator does not establish a universal lender CLTV requirement or determine whether you qualify for a HELOC.

A HELOC is secured by your home. Failure to repay can put the property at risk, and lenders may reduce or freeze access to additional credit under certain circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator estimate a HELOC payment during the draw period?

It calculates an interest-only estimate for one month using your outstanding balance and entered APR. Your lender's actual minimum payment may include different principal, interest, fee, or minimum-payment requirements.

How is the repayment payment calculated?

The balance entering repayment is amortized using the monthly rate and selected repayment term. The formula calculates a level principal-and-interest payment when those assumptions remain unchanged.

Why might my actual HELOC payment be different?

HELOCs commonly use variable interest rates, and your balance may change as you borrow or repay money. Lender payment rules, fees, rate adjustments, and repayment terms can also change the amount due.

Is the Planning max CLTV percentage a lending rule?

No. It is an input for your planning scenario. Actual CLTV requirements and underwriting standards depend on the lender, loan program, property valuation, and borrower circumstances.

Does the HELOC Calculator include fees?

The documented calculation uses the balance, APR, repayment term, and equity-related inputs shown in the calculator. It does not separately calculate lender fees, closing costs, annual fees, or transaction charges.

Can this calculator tell me whether I will qualify for a HELOC?

No. It estimates financial results from the numbers you enter. It does not evaluate income, credit history, debt-to-income ratio, property eligibility, lender underwriting rules, or loan approval.

Can my HELOC credit availability change after opening the account?

Yes. CFPB guidance explains that lenders may sometimes reduce or freeze additional borrowing, including when property values or a borrower's financial circumstances change.

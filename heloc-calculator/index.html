What Is a HELOC Calculator?

A HELOC calculator estimates payments and borrowing capacity for a home equity line of credit. CFPB defines a HELOC as an open-end line of credit that lets a homeowner borrow repeatedly against available home equity during the draw period.

Draw-Period Payment Estimate

This calculator shows an interest-only estimate for the current balance because many HELOCs permit minimum payments tied to the outstanding balance during the draw period. Your actual plan may require principal payments, minimum-dollar payments, fees, or other terms.

Interest-Only Payment ≈ Current HELOC Balance × APR ÷ 12

Repayment-Period Estimate

The repayment result assumes the current balance is frozen at the start of repayment and amortized over the selected repayment term at the current APR. Because most HELOCs have variable rates, the real payment can be higher or lower when rates change.

Available Equity and CLTV

The calculator uses your own planning CLTV ceiling to estimate additional room. Lenders may use different CLTV limits, appraisal methods, credit requirements, and minimum line sizes.

Risk and Limitations

A HELOC is secured by your home. CFPB warns that failure to repay can put the home at risk, and a lender may reduce or freeze access to additional credit in some circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HELOC?

A HELOC is an open-end line of credit secured by home equity. It generally allows repeated borrowing up to a credit limit during a draw period.

Are HELOC rates fixed?

CFPB says HELOCs usually have variable interest rates, so payments can change. Some products may allow part of the balance to be converted to a fixed rate.

Why can payments rise after the draw period?

When the draw period ends, new borrowing stops and the account enters repayment. Payments can increase because the borrower may then need to repay principal as well as interest over a limited term.

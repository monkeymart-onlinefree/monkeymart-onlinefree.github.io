What Is a Compound Interest Calculator?

Answer: This compound-interest calculator converts a nominal annual rate from one compounding frequency to another while preserving the same effective annual rate.

How to Use the Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the nominal annual rate.
  2. Select how often that rate compounds.
  3. Select the target compounding frequency.
  4. Convert and compare equivalent nominal rates.

Effective Annual Rate Formula

For m compounding periods per year:

EAR = (1 + j/m)^m − 1

For continuous compounding:

EAR = e^j − 1

The calculator then solves the formula backward for the target compounding frequency.

Calculation Example

A 6% nominal annual rate compounded monthly has a higher effective annual rate than 6% compounded annually. The equivalent quarterly nominal rate is adjusted so both choices produce the same one-year growth.

Assumptions and Limitations

This is a rate-conversion tool. It does not model contributions, withdrawals, taxes, fees, or investment volatility. Use the Interest Calculator for balance projections.

Compound Interest Calculator FAQs

What is a nominal annual rate?

A nominal annual rate states an annualized rate before fully accounting for within-year compounding.

What is the effective annual rate?

The effective annual rate is the actual one-year growth rate after compounding is considered.

Why do equivalent nominal rates differ?

More frequent compounding changes how often interest is added, so the nominal rate must change to preserve the same effective annual growth.

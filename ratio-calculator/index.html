What Is a Ratio Calculator?

A ratio calculator solves proportions in the form A:B = C:D. If any three terms are known, the fourth can be found using the fact that equivalent ratios have equal cross products.

How to Use the Ratio Calculator

Enter values for any three of A, B, C, and D and leave exactly one field blank. Select Solve Proportion. The calculator fills the relationship numerically and shows the decimal value of each side.

Ratio and Proportion Formula

A:B = C:D is equivalent to A/B = C/D, provided B and D are not zero. For a true proportion, the cross products are equal: A × D = B × C.

Calculation Example

For 2:3 = 10:D, cross multiplication gives 2D = 30. Dividing by 2 gives D = 15, so 2:3 = 10:15.

Ratios vs. Rates

A ratio compares two quantities by division. A rate is a ratio that often compares quantities with different units, such as kilometers per hour. A proportion states that two ratios or rates are equal.

Limitations

The calculator solves one four-term proportion at a time. It does not automatically verify unit compatibility, so make sure corresponding numerators and denominators represent compatible quantities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you solve a missing value in a ratio?

Write the relationship as A/B = C/D, set the cross products equal, and isolate the missing variable.

Can the denominator of a ratio be zero?

No. A fraction-style ratio with zero in the denominator is undefined, so B and D must be nonzero.

Are 2:3 and 10:15 equivalent?

Yes. Both ratios equal two-thirds, and their cross products are equal: 2 × 15 = 3 × 10.

Do ratios need units?

Ratios can compare quantities with the same or different units, but the units should be interpreted consistently. A rate such as km/h keeps its units meaningful.

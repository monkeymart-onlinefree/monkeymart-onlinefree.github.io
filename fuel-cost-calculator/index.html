What Is a Fuel Cost Calculator?

A fuel cost calculator estimates how much fuel a trip will consume and how much that fuel will cost. The core inputs are trip distance, vehicle fuel efficiency, and the price paid per gallon or liter. It is useful for road-trip planning, commuting budgets, delivery estimates, and comparing vehicles.

How to Use the Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Enter the one-way trip distance and select miles or kilometers.
  2. Enter your vehicle's fuel efficiency as MPG, L/100 km, or km/L.
  3. Enter the fuel price and choose whether it is priced per US gallon or per liter.
  4. Enable round trip when you want the return journey included.

Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel used = Distance ÷ Fuel economy
Fuel cost = Fuel used × Fuel price

For MPG, distance and fuel economy must use compatible mile/gallon units. For L/100 km, liters used equal distance in kilometers × L/100 km ÷ 100.

Example

A 300-mile trip in a vehicle that averages 30 MPG uses 10 US gallons. At $3.50 per gallon, the estimated fuel cost is $35.00.

Understanding the Result

The result is a fuel-only estimate. Real consumption can change with traffic, speed, tire pressure, weather, elevation, payload, idling, route choice, and driving style.

Limitations

The calculator assumes the entered fuel economy is representative of the trip. It does not include tolls, parking, maintenance, depreciation, insurance, charging costs for plug-in vehicles, or currency conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Divide trip distance by vehicle fuel economy to estimate fuel used, then multiply fuel used by the fuel price. Make sure the units match.

Does this calculator support liters per 100 km?

Yes. Choose L/100 km for fuel efficiency and kilometers for distance. The calculator converts inputs internally before estimating fuel used and trip cost.

Is fuel cost the same as total driving cost?

No. Fuel cost excludes depreciation, maintenance, insurance, tolls, parking, financing, and other ownership or trip expenses.

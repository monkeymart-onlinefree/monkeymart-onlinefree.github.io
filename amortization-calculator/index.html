What Is an Amortization Calculator?

Answer: An amortization calculator shows how each scheduled payment is divided between interest and principal and how the outstanding balance changes over time.

How to Use the Amortization Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount, interest rate, and term.
  2. Optionally enter an extra monthly payment.
  3. Calculate the required payment.
  4. Review the payoff time, interest, and schedule.

How Amortization Works

The required payment is calculated with the fixed-rate loan formula. Each month:

Interest = Opening Balance × Monthly Rate Principal = Payment − Interest New Balance = Opening Balance − Principal

Calculation Example

An extra $200 per month on a fixed-rate loan goes toward principal after current-period interest is covered. Lower principal means less interest is charged in later months.

Assumptions and Limitations

The extra-payment model assumes no prepayment penalty and that the lender applies the extra amount directly to principal. Confirm how your lender handles additional payments.

Amortization Calculator FAQs

What is amortization?

Amortization is the gradual repayment of a debt through payments that cover interest and reduce principal.

Do extra payments always save interest?

On a standard interest-bearing amortizing loan, reducing principal earlier generally reduces future interest, assuming no prepayment fee or unusual loan terms.

Why is more of the early payment interest?

Early in the loan, the balance is higher, so the interest charge calculated on that balance is also higher.

What Is a Mileage Calculator?

This standalone mileage calculator measures trip distance from two odometer readings. If you also enter average speed, fuel economy, and fuel price, it estimates trip time, fuel required, and fuel cost. It is designed to work without external mapping services.

Mileage and Trip Formulas

Distance = Ending Odometer − Starting Odometer
Time = Distance ÷ Average Speed
Fuel (mpg) = Miles ÷ mpg
Fuel (L/100 km) = km × (L/100 km) ÷ 100
Fuel Cost = Fuel × Price

Calculation Example

If an odometer moves from 24,500 to 24,780 miles, the trip distance is 280 miles. At an average 55 mph, estimated moving time is about 5.09 hours before stops. At 30 mpg, estimated fuel is about 9.33 gallons.

Odometer Method vs. Route Distance

An odometer measures distance actually traveled by the vehicle. Address-to-address route calculators use road-network data and can change with route choice, closures, or map updates. This page intentionally uses recorded or planned odometer-style distance.

Limitations

Actual travel time depends on traffic, stops, weather, and road conditions. Fuel economy varies with speed, load, terrain, tires, temperature, and driving behavior. Odometers and vehicle fuel estimates also have measurement error.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this mileage calculator find trip distance?

It subtracts the starting odometer reading from the ending odometer reading. That works offline and does not require a map or geocoding service.

Can it estimate travel time?

Yes. If you enter a positive average speed, the calculator divides trip distance by average speed. Actual time can differ because of traffic, stops, road conditions, and speed changes.

How is fuel use estimated?

For miles, fuel use is distance divided by mpg. For kilometers, it uses distance × L/100 km ÷ 100. Enter your vehicle's realistic average fuel economy for the trip.

Is this the same as a route planner?

No. It does not choose roads or calculate address-to-address distance. Use a mapping service for routing; use this page for odometer-based trip mileage, time, fuel, and cost estimates.

Tips and common mistakes

Do not mix incompatible units without converting them first. The most frequent errors are mixing units — such as entering centimetres where metres are expected — and typing a percentage as a decimal or the other way around. Enter values exactly as the labels describe them, keep every measurement in one system, and check the result against a rough mental estimate. If the two are far apart, the input is usually the cause.

Accuracy and limitations

Results are calculated exactly from the values you enter, so the accuracy of the output depends on the accuracy and units of your inputs.

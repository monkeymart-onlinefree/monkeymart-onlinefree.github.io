What Is Simple Interest?

Answer: Simple interest calculates interest only on the original principal. Unlike compound interest, previously accumulated interest does not itself earn interest.

How to Use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Choose the value you want to solve.
  2. Enter the other three values.
  3. Use an annual rate as a percentage and time in years.
  4. Calculate the missing value.

Simple Interest Formula

I = P × r × t A = P + I

P is principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, t is time in years, I is interest, and A is the final amount.

Calculation Example

$10,000 at 5% simple interest for 3 years earns $1,500 of interest, for a final amount of $11,500.

Assumptions and Limitations

Many real savings products and loans compound rather than use simple interest over the full term. Confirm the contract’s interest method before applying this formula.

Simple Interest Calculator FAQs

Does simple interest compound?

No. Interest is based on the original principal only.

How do I convert 5% to the formula rate?

Divide by 100, so 5% becomes 0.05.

Can I solve for time?

Yes. Rearranging the formula gives t = I ÷ (P×r).

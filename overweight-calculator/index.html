What is an Overweight Calculator?

An Overweight Calculator typically uses body mass index (BMI) to compare an adult’s weight with height. For adults 20 and older, CDC categories classify BMI below 18.5 as underweight, 18.5 to less than 25 as healthy weight, 25 to less than 30 as overweight, and 30 or greater as obesity.

BMI formula

Metric: BMI = kg ÷ m². US customary: BMI = 703 × lb ÷ in². The calculator also rearranges the same formula to show the weights that correspond to BMI 18.5 and 24.9 for the entered height.

Important limitations

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and does not measure fat distribution. It may be less informative for some individuals and should not be treated as a diagnosis. Children and teenagers require BMI-for-age percentiles rather than the adult categories on this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI 25 overweight?

Under the standard CDC adult categories, BMI 25.0 is the beginning of the overweight range.

Can a muscular person have a high BMI?

Yes. BMI can be high because of greater lean mass, so it cannot distinguish muscle from body fat.

Why is age limited to 20+ here?

CDC adult BMI categories are specified for adults 20 and older. Younger people should use age- and sex-specific growth charts.

Is the “healthy range” a personal goal?

No. It is only the weight interval corresponding mathematically to standard BMI 18.5–24.9 for that height, not an individualized medical target.

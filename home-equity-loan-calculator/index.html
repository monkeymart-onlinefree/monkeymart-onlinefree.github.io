What Is a Home Equity Loan Calculator?

A home equity loan calculator estimates how a lump-sum loan secured by your home would affect your monthly payment and combined loan-to-value ratio. CFPB describes a home equity loan as borrowing against home equity, usually through a lump-sum loan that commonly has a fixed interest rate.

Home Equity and CLTV Formulas

Home Equity = Home Value − Existing Mortgage Balance
CLTV = (Existing Mortgage + New Equity Loan) ÷ Home Value × 100

The “planning max CLTV” is deliberately editable because lenders set their own limits. The available-equity result is the amount remaining under that user-selected CLTV ceiling, not a promise of credit approval.

Monthly Payment Formula

The new home equity loan is modeled as a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan. The payment covers principal and interest over the selected term. Existing first-mortgage payments are not included in this calculator.

Important Risk

A home equity loan uses the home as collateral. CFPB notes that failure to repay can put the home at risk of foreclosure. Compare total costs and not only the monthly payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is home equity calculated?

Home equity is the current property value minus debt already secured by the property. A home equity loan borrows against part of that equity.

Is a home equity loan usually fixed-rate?

CFPB says a home equity loan usually has a fixed interest rate and is paid to the borrower as a lump sum. Product terms can vary by lender.

What is CLTV?

Combined loan-to-value compares total debt secured by the property, including the first mortgage and new home equity loan, with the property value.

Use the Home Equity Loan Calculator to estimate the monthly principal-and-interest payment on a new home equity loan, along with your current home equity, planning CLTV, available borrowing room, and total interest. Enter your home value, current mortgage balance, desired equity loan, interest rate, loan term, and planning maximum CLTV.

A home equity loan lets you borrow against your home equity and generally provides the borrowed amount as a lump sum. If another mortgage is already secured by the property, the home equity loan is generally a second mortgage or junior lien.

What Is the Home Equity Loan Calculator?

The Home Equity Loan Calculator is a planning tool for estimating the cost of a fixed-rate home equity loan and how the new loan would affect the debt secured by your home.

It calculates the new loan's monthly principal-and-interest payment using a standard amortizing-loan method. It also helps compare your desired loan with your current equity and a planning maximum combined loan-to-value, or CLTV, percentage.

The calculator does not determine whether a lender will approve your application. Actual underwriting may consider property valuation, credit, income, debt obligations, lender policies, fees, and other factors.

How to Use the Home Equity Loan Calculator

Enter the following information:

Home Value: Enter the current estimated value of your property.

Current Mortgage Balance: Enter the amount you currently owe on the existing mortgage secured by the home.

Desired Equity Loan: Enter the amount you want to borrow with the new home equity loan.

Interest Rate (%): Enter the annual interest rate for the proposed home equity loan.

Term (Years): Enter the number of years over which the new loan will be repaid.

Planning Max CLTV (%): Enter the maximum combined loan-to-value percentage you want to use for planning. This is an assumption entered by you, not a lender approval threshold.

After entering the values, use the calculated results to review the payment, interest cost, equity position, borrowing room, and resulting CLTV.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

The new home equity loan is modeled as a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan with monthly principal-and-interest payments:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

CFPB explains that fixed-rate mortgage principal-and-interest payments depend on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term and are calculated so regular scheduled payments repay the loan by the end of the term.

Variables

M = monthly principal-and-interest payment on the new home equity loan

P = desired home equity loan principal

r = monthly interest rate

n = total number of monthly payments

The monthly rate is:

r = Annual Interest Rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12

The number of payments is:

n = Term in Years × 12

Calculation Sequence

  1. Determine current equity from the entered home value and current mortgage balance.
  2. Calculate the borrowing room under the user-entered planning maximum CLTV.
  3. Calculate the CLTV after adding the desired home equity loan.
  4. Convert the annual interest rate to a monthly decimal rate.
  5. Convert the loan term from years to monthly payment periods.
  6. Apply the fixed-rate amortization formula to the desired equity loan.
  7. Multiply the monthly payment by the number of payments to estimate total scheduled principal and interest.
  8. Subtract the original home equity loan principal to estimate total interest.

Current Home Equity

Current home equity is:

Current Equity = Home Value − Current Mortgage Balance

CFPB similarly defines home equity as the home's current value minus the amount owed on the existing mortgage.

Planning Borrowing Room

Using the maximum CLTV entered by the user:

Planning Borrowing Room = (Home Value × Planning Max CLTV) − Current Mortgage Balance

For example, an 80% input is converted to 0.80 before multiplication.

This is a planning estimate. It does not mean a lender will approve that amount.

Combined Loan-to-Value

The calculator's planning CLTV after adding the desired home equity loan can be expressed as:

CLTV = (Current Mortgage Balance + Desired Equity Loan) ÷ Home Value × 100

CLTV generally compares multiple loans secured by the same property with the property's value. Formal lender underwriting conventions can differ depending on the transaction and loan program.

Total Scheduled Payment

Total Scheduled Payment = Monthly Payment × Number of Payments

This represents principal and interest on the new home equity loan over the selected term.

Total Interest

Total Interest = Total Scheduled Payment − Desired Equity Loan

This assumes the rate remains fixed and every scheduled payment is made as modeled.

Worked Example

Inputs

Home value: $500,000

Current mortgage balance: $250,000

Desired equity loan: $75,000

Annual interest rate: 8.00%

Term: 15 years

Planning max CLTV: 80%

Step 1: Calculate Current Equity

$500,000 − $250,000 = $250,000

Current home equity is $250,000.

Step 2: Calculate Planning Borrowing Room

Convert 80% to 0.80:

$500,000 × 0.80 = $400,000

Then subtract the current mortgage balance:

$400,000 − $250,000 = $150,000

Planning borrowing room is $150,000 under the selected 80% CLTV assumption.

Step 3: Calculate CLTV After the New Loan

Combined secured debt:

$250,000 + $75,000 = $325,000

CLTV:

$325,000 ÷ $500,000 × 100 = 65%

The estimated planning CLTV is 65%.

Step 4: Calculate the Monthly Rate and Number of Payments

Monthly interest rate:

r = 0.08 ÷ 12 = 0.0066666667

Number of payments:

n = 15 × 12 = 180

Step 5: Calculate the Monthly Payment

M = $75,000 × [0.0066666667 × (1.0066666667)^180] ÷ [(1.0066666667)^180 − 1]
M ≈ $716.74

The estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment on the new home equity loan is $716.74.

Step 6: Calculate Total Interest

Using the unrounded monthly result:

Total scheduled payments ≈ $129,013.03
Total interest = $129,013.03 − $75,000
Total interest ≈ $54,013.03

Final Result

For this example:

  • Current equity: $250,000
  • Planning borrowing room: $150,000
  • CLTV after desired loan: 65%
  • New home equity loan payment: about $716.74 per month
  • Total interest over 15 years: about $54,013.03

Understanding Your Results

Monthly Payment: The estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment for the new home equity loan only. It does not include the payment on your existing first mortgage.

Current Equity: The difference between the entered home value and current mortgage balance.

Available Borrowing Room: The amount of additional secured debt that fits within the planning maximum CLTV you entered. This is an estimate, not an approval amount.

CLTV: The percentage of the entered home value represented by the current mortgage balance plus the desired equity loan.

Total Interest: The estimated interest paid on the new loan if the modeled payment, rate, and term remain unchanged.

A lender's actual payment or total borrowing cost can be higher because home equity loans may involve upfront fees and other costs not represented by principal-and-interest payments alone.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator models the desired home equity loan as a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan with regular monthly principal-and-interest payments.

The existing first-mortgage payment is not included in the calculated home equity loan payment.

Home value is based entirely on the amount entered. A lender may use an appraisal or another approved property valuation when making an actual lending decision.

The planning max CLTV is a user-entered assumption. The calculator does not apply a universal lender CLTV limit or guarantee that the selected percentage is available to you.

The calculation does not determine credit eligibility, debt-to-income qualification, income requirements, lender underwriting, approval status, or final loan terms.

Fees and other borrowing costs are not separate calculator inputs. CFPB notes that home equity loans can have upfront fees and costs, so borrowers should compare more than the monthly payment.

Displayed results may be rounded for readability. Calculations should retain greater precision internally before the final values are rounded.

Because a home equity loan is secured by the property, failure to repay can put the home at risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a home equity loan calculator calculate?

It estimates the monthly principal-and-interest payment for a proposed home equity loan and helps evaluate current equity, planning borrowing room, CLTV, and total interest.

Does the monthly payment include my existing mortgage?

No. The payment result applies to the new home equity loan. Your existing first-mortgage payment is not added to it.

What is the difference between home equity and CLTV?

Home equity is the home's value minus the current mortgage balance. CLTV measures secured loan balances relative to the property's value after considering additional financing.

Is the planning max CLTV an approval limit?

No. It is an input used for scenario planning. Actual lending limits and underwriting requirements depend on the lender, loan product, property, borrower, and applicable rules.

Why can my lender's payment differ from the calculator?

Differences may result from the final approved loan amount, interest rate, term, lender calculations, property valuation, fees, closing costs, or other loan terms.

Does a higher loan term reduce the payment?

With the same principal and interest rate, spreading repayment over more months generally lowers the required monthly principal-and-interest payment, but it can increase total interest paid because interest accrues over a longer period.

Is a home equity loan the same as a HELOC?

No. A home equity loan generally provides a specific lump sum that is repaid over time. A HELOC is a revolving line of credit that can allow repeated borrowing up to an available credit limit during its draw period.

What Is a Lease Calculator?

This lease calculator values a simple general asset lease using present-value mathematics. In payment mode, it solves the level monthly payment from asset value, residual value, term, and annual rate. In rate mode, it reverses the cash flow to estimate the annual rate implied by a known payment.

Lease Payment Formula

Asset value = Payment × [1 − (1 + r)−n] ÷ r + Residual ÷ (1 + r)n

Solving this equation for Payment gives the level end-of-month lease payment. The residual is discounted back from the end of the lease.

What Is Residual Value?

Residual value is the assumed value of the asset at the end of the lease term. A larger residual generally reduces the amount that must be recovered through periodic lease payments, although the financing rate still affects the payment.

Limitations

This is a finance-math model, not a legal or accounting lease classification tool. It excludes taxes, acquisition fees, deposits, maintenance, insurance, purchase-option fees, and payment timing other than end-of-month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this calculate an auto lease?

The mathematics can model a simple asset lease, but auto leases often use money factors, taxes, rebates, acquisition fees, and jurisdiction-specific rules. A dedicated auto lease model may be more appropriate.

What does Find Rate do?

It solves for the monthly discount rate that makes the present value of the entered lease payments and residual equal the asset value, then annualizes that monthly rate by multiplying by 12.

Does the calculator assume payments at the beginning or end of the month?

It assumes payments at the end of each month. Beginning-of-period payments would have a different present value.

What Is a Triangle Calculator?

Answer: A triangle calculator solves unknown sides and angles when enough independent measurements are known. This version supports SSS, SAS, and ASA/AAS cases.

How to Use the Triangle Calculator

  1. Select SSS, SAS, or ASA/AAS.
  2. Enter the required measurements.
  3. Calculate the missing sides and angles.
  4. Review perimeter, area, and triangle classification.

Triangle Laws and Formulas

Law of cosines: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos(A) Law of sines: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C) Heron area: √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]

Calculation Example

A 3-4-5 triangle has a perimeter of 12, area of 6, and one 90° angle.

Assumptions and Limitations

This version covers common unambiguous SSS, SAS, and two-angle cases. It does not implement the ambiguous SSA case.

Triangle Calculator FAQs

What is the triangle inequality?

The sum of any two side lengths must be greater than the third side.

Do triangle angles always total 180 degrees?

For an ordinary Euclidean triangle, the three interior angles total 180°.

What is SSS?

SSS means all three side lengths are known.

What Is a Due Date Calculator?

Answer: A due date calculator estimates the date a pregnancy reaches 40 weeks. The conventional LMP method uses 280 days from the first day of the last menstrual period, while conception and IVF dates use different offsets.

How to Use the Due Date Calculator

  1. Select LMP, conception date, or IVF transfer.
  2. Enter the relevant date and any cycle/embryo information.
  3. Calculate the EDD.
  4. Use your clinician’s established obstetric due date for medical decisions.

Due Date Formulas

LMP: EDD ≈ LMP + 280 days + cycle adjustment Conception: EDD ≈ conception + 266 days Day-5 IVF transfer: EDD = transfer + 261 days Day-3 IVF transfer: EDD = transfer + 263 days

Calculation Example

For a day-5 embryo transfer, ACOG gives an example rule of assigning the EDD 261 days from the transfer date.

Assumptions and Limitations

EDD is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. ACOG recommends using ART-derived gestational age for IVF pregnancies and prioritizes accurate early ultrasound for establishing or confirming gestational age.

Due Date Calculator FAQs

Why is LMP due date 280 days?

By convention, a 40-week pregnancy is counted as 280 days from the first day of the LMP.

How is IVF dating different?

ACOG recommends ART-derived gestational age. For example, a day-5 embryo transfer corresponds to an EDD 261 days after transfer.

Does every pregnancy deliver on the due date?

No. The EDD is a clinical reference point, not a prediction of the exact delivery day.

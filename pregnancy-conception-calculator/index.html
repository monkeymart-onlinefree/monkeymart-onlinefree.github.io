What Is a Pregnancy Conception Calculator?

Answer: A pregnancy conception calculator works backward from an estimated due date or forward from the last menstrual period to estimate when conception may have occurred. It cannot determine an exact biological conception day in most naturally conceived pregnancies.

How to Use the Pregnancy Conception Calculator

  1. Select whether you know the due date or LMP.
  2. Enter the relevant date.
  3. For LMP, enter the average cycle length.
  4. Review the estimated conception date and nearby calendar window.

Conception-Date Estimate

From EDD: conception ≈ EDD − 266 days From LMP: conception ≈ LMP + 14 days + (cycle length − 28)

Calculation Example

If an EDD is October 8, the simple calendar estimate places conception about 266 days earlier. LMP-based estimates shift with the entered cycle length.

Assumptions and Limitations

Ovulation can occur earlier or later than a simple cycle estimate, sperm can remain viable for several days, and fertilization/implantation timing varies. Clinical dating is based on obstetric methods rather than retrospective certainty about intercourse dates.

Pregnancy Conception Calculator FAQs

Why subtract 266 days from the due date?

An EDD is conventionally about 280 days from the LMP, while conception in a typical 28-day cycle is estimated about 14 days after the LMP, leaving about 266 days from conception to EDD.

Can this prove the exact conception date?

No. Calendar estimates cannot usually identify an exact conception day.

Does cycle length affect the LMP estimate?

Yes. A longer or shorter average cycle can shift the estimated ovulation and conception date.

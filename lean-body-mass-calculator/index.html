What Is Lean Body Mass?

Lean body mass (LBM) is total body weight minus body-fat weight. It includes muscle, bone, organs, body water, connective tissue, and other non-fat components.

Lean Body Mass Formulas

For adults, this calculator shows three commonly used equations. Weight W is in kilograms and height H is in centimeters.

Boer (male): 0.407W + 0.267H − 19.2
Boer (female): 0.252W + 0.473H − 48.3

James (male): 1.1W − 128(W/H)²
James (female): 1.07W − 148(W/H)²

Hume (male): 0.32810W + 0.33929H − 29.5336
Hume (female): 0.29569W + 0.41813H − 43.2933

Why Show Three Results?

These are population-derived equations, not direct measurements. Comparing multiple formulas makes the uncertainty more visible. The three-formula average on this page is a convenience summary, not a medical standard.

Limitations

Equation estimates can be less representative at unusual heights, weights, or body compositions. They cannot distinguish a muscular athlete from a sedentary person with the same height and weight. Clinical uses may require a specific equation or measured body composition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does lean body mass include?

Lean body mass is body weight minus fat mass. It includes muscle, bone, organs, water, skin, and other non-fat tissues.

Why do different lean body mass formulas give different results?

Boer, James, and Hume are regression equations developed from different datasets and mathematical forms, so they can produce different estimates for the same person.

Is a formula the same as a DEXA measurement?

No. Height-and-weight equations estimate lean mass from population relationships. A direct body-composition method such as DEXA measures body compartments more specifically.

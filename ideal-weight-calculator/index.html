What Is an Ideal Weight Calculator?

Answer: An ideal weight calculator compares height-based formulas that estimate an ideal body weight (IBW). These formulas were developed for practical clinical uses and do not define a universally healthy or appropriate weight for every person.

How to Use the Ideal Weight Calculator

  1. Select the sex used by the historical formula.
  2. Enter height in centimeters.
  3. Compare Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi estimates.
  4. Also review the separate adult BMI 18.5–24.9 weight range.

Ideal Body Weight Formulas

Devine: Men 50 + 2.3 kg/in over 5 ft; Women 45.5 + 2.3 Robinson: Men 52 + 1.9; Women 49 + 1.7 Miller: Men 56.2 + 1.41; Women 53.1 + 1.36 Hamwi: Men 48 + 2.7; Women 45.5 + 2.2

Calculation Example

At 175 cm, the calculator converts height to inches, finds inches above 5 feet, and applies each formula separately. The displayed average is only a comparison summary.

Assumptions and Limitations

These formulas do not account for body composition, frame size, age-related changes, pregnancy, ethnicity, athletic build, or individual clinical needs.

Ideal Weight Calculator FAQs

Which ideal-weight formula is best?

There is no single universally best formula. The page shows several historical formulas so their differences are visible.

Is ideal body weight the same as healthy weight?

No. IBW formulas and BMI-based healthy-weight ranges are different concepts and both have limitations.

Why is height under 5 feet restricted here?

The classic formulas are commonly expressed as a base weight at 5 feet plus an increment for each inch over 5 feet.

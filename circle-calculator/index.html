What Is a Circle Calculator?

A circle calculator finds radius, diameter, circumference, and area from any one known circle measurement. Because these quantities are linked by π, one positive value is enough to determine the others.

How to Use the Circle Calculator

Enter exactly one known measurement: radius, diameter, circumference, or area. Leave the other fields blank and select Calculate Circle. The unit menu labels the length and squared-area outputs; it does not convert between units.

Circle Formulas

Diameter d = 2r. Circumference C = 2πr = πd. Area A = πr². If area is known, radius is √(A/π).

Calculation Example

For radius r = 5, diameter = 10, circumference = 10π ≈ 31.4159, and area = 25π ≈ 78.5398.

Understanding π

Pi is the constant ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. The calculator uses the JavaScript Math.PI value rather than a short decimal such as 3.14.

Limitations

The formulas apply to ideal Euclidean circles. The unit selector only labels outputs, so enter the known quantity in the unit you want the length results to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find a circle's diameter from radius?

Multiply the radius by 2: d = 2r.

How do you find circumference from diameter?

Multiply the diameter by π: C = πd.

How do you find radius from area?

Divide the area by π and take the square root: r = √(A/π).

Does the unit selector convert measurements?

No. It labels the output. Enter the input in the desired unit.

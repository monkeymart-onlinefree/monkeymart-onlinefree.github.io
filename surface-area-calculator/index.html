What Is a Surface Area Calculator?

A surface area calculator finds the total area covering the outside of a three-dimensional solid. This tool includes common solids such as spheres, cones, cubes, closed cylinders, rectangular prisms, capsules, conical frustums, and square pyramids.

How to Use the Surface Area Calculator

Choose a solid, enter its required dimensions, and select Calculate Surface Area. The result uses squared versions of the chosen unit label. For compound surfaces such as cones or cylinders, the calculator can also show component areas.

Common Surface Area Formulas

Sphere: 4πr². Cube: 6a². Closed cylinder: 2πr² + 2πrh. Rectangular prism: 2lw + 2lh + 2wh. Closed cone: πr² + πr√(r²+h²).

Capsule, Frustum, and Pyramid

For a capsule with cylindrical section height h, SA = 4πr² + 2πrh. A closed conical frustum uses the two end circles plus its lateral area. A square pyramid uses its square base plus four triangular faces.

Calculation Example

A closed cylinder with r = 3 and h = 5 has surface area 2π(3²) + 2π(3)(5) = 48π ≈ 150.796 square units.

Units and Limitations

Surface area is expressed in square units. The unit menu labels results but does not convert dimensions. Formulas assume ideal geometric solids and positive linear dimensions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the surface area of a sphere?

For radius r, the sphere's surface area is 4πr².

Does cylinder surface area include both circular ends?

The closed-cylinder option on this page includes both circular ends plus the lateral surface.

What does capsule height mean here?

For the capsule calculation, h is the length of the cylindrical middle section. The two hemispherical ends together contribute the same surface area as a sphere.

Why are the results in square units?

Surface area measures two-dimensional coverage over a 3D object's exterior, so a length unit such as cm becomes cm².

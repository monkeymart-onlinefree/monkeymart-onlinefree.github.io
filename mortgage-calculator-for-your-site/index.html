What Is an Embeddable Mortgage Calculator?

This tool calculates the scheduled monthly principal-and-interest payment for a fully amortizing fixed-rate loan, then adds optional annual property tax, PMI, and other costs as monthly equivalents. The iframe section helps you place the tool on another page.

Mortgage Payment Formula

M = P × r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments. When the rate is zero, the payment is simply principal divided by the number of months.

What the Monthly Total Includes

The displayed monthly total equals principal and interest plus one-twelfth of the annual tax, PMI, and other-cost inputs. It does not automatically estimate insurance, closing costs, HOA dues, rate changes, or escrow adjustments unless you enter them in the available cost fields.

How to Use It on Your Website

Host this HTML file on your domain, copy the iframe code, and paste it into the HTML area of the page where you want the calculator. Update the iframe URL if you generated the code while viewing the file locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator include property tax?

Yes, if you enter an annual property-tax amount. The calculator divides it by 12 and adds it to the estimated monthly total.

Does it support a 0% interest rate?

Yes. At 0% interest, the scheduled principal-and-interest payment is the loan amount divided by the number of monthly payments.

Is this an APR calculator?

No. The interest-rate input is used as a nominal annual rate divided by 12 for the standard fixed-rate payment formula. Fees that affect APR are not modeled.

Can I embed it on a commercial site?

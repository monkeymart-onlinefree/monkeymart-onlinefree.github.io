Use the VAT Calculator to add VAT to a net price or understand how VAT can be removed from a VAT-inclusive gross price. Enter a VAT rate, currency symbol, and net price to calculate the VAT amount and VAT-inclusive value.

What Is the VAT Calculator?

The VAT Calculator helps calculate Value Added Tax using a custom percentage rate. It is useful when you need to find the VAT added to a price before tax or understand the reverse calculation from a VAT-inclusive amount.

VAT is calculated from a taxable amount using the applicable VAT rate. The exact VAT rate and tax treatment can vary by country, product, transaction, and date.

How to Use the VAT Calculator

  1. VAT rate (%) — Enter the VAT percentage you want to use, such as 5, 15, or 20.
  2. Currency symbol — Enter or select the symbol used to display the monetary result. This affects display only and does not convert currencies.
  3. Net price (excluding VAT) — Enter the price before VAT is added.
  4. Calculate the result to see the VAT amount and VAT-inclusive price.

Use the VAT rate that actually applies to your transaction. The calculator does not determine the legally correct VAT rate for you.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

First convert the VAT percentage to decimal form:

r = R ÷ 100

To add VAT to a net price:

Gross Price = Net Price × (1 + r)

The VAT amount is:

VAT = Gross Price − Net Price

This can also be written as:

VAT = Net Price × r

Official VAT guidance uses the same principle: VAT is calculated by multiplying the tax-exclusive value by the applicable tax rate.

Removing VAT from a Gross Price

When the gross price already includes VAT:

Net Price = Gross Price ÷ (1 + r)

Then:

VAT = Gross Price − Net Price

Official guidance similarly calculates a VAT-exclusive price by dividing the VAT-inclusive price by the appropriate VAT factor.

VAT Fraction of a Gross Amount

The fraction of a VAT-inclusive price that represents VAT is:

VAT Fraction = r ÷ (1 + r)

Using a percentage rate directly:

VAT Fraction = R ÷ (100 + R)

Therefore:

VAT = Gross Price × [R ÷ (100 + R)]

HMRC describes this VAT-fraction method for calculating tax already included in a gross amount.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Convert the entered VAT percentage into a decimal.
  2. Multiply the net price by the decimal VAT rate to calculate VAT.
  3. Add the VAT amount to the net price to calculate the gross price.
  4. For the reverse calculation, divide a gross amount by 1 + r to recover the net amount.
  5. Calculate VAT as the difference between gross and net.

Worked Example

Assume a net price of $200 and a custom VAT rate of 15%.

Inputs

  • VAT rate: 15%
  • Currency symbol: $
  • Net price: $200

Step 1: Convert the VAT Rate

r = 15 ÷ 100
r = 0.15

Step 2: Calculate VAT

VAT = $200 × 0.15
VAT = $30

Step 3: Calculate the Gross Price

Gross Price = $200 × (1 + 0.15)
Gross Price = $230

Final Result

Result Amount
Net Price $200.00
VAT $30.00
Gross Price $230.00

The reverse calculation also confirms the result:

$230 ÷ 1.15 = $200

So the VAT included in the $230 gross amount is:

$230 − $200 = $30

Understanding Your Results

Net Price is the amount before VAT is added.

VAT Amount is the tax calculated from the net price and VAT rate.

Gross Price is the total amount after VAT has been added.

The currency symbol only controls how monetary values are displayed. It does not perform exchange-rate conversion.

Assumptions and Limitations

  • The calculator uses the VAT percentage entered by the user.
  • It does not decide which VAT rate legally applies to a transaction.
  • VAT rates, exemptions, zero-rating, reverse-charge rules, and place-of-supply rules vary by jurisdiction and transaction.
  • The calculator does not determine whether a product or service is VAT-exempt or outside the scope of VAT.
  • Currency selection or the currency symbol does not convert money between currencies.
  • Actual invoices may differ slightly because tax authorities or businesses can apply specific invoice or line-item rounding rules.
  • The calculated result is mathematical guidance and is not tax, accounting, or legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add VAT to a net price?

Convert the VAT percentage to a decimal and multiply the net price by 1 + the decimal VAT rate.

For example, with a 15% rate:

Gross = Net × 1.15
How do I calculate the VAT amount only?

For a VAT-exclusive net price:

VAT = Net Price × VAT Rate

The VAT rate must first be expressed as a decimal.

Why can’t I remove 20% VAT by subtracting 20% from the gross price?

Because the 20% VAT rate was calculated from the smaller net price, not from the final gross price.

For a gross price containing 20% VAT:

Net = Gross ÷ 1.20

The VAT portion of the gross amount is:

20 ÷ 120 = 1/6, or about 16.67% of the gross price.
Does the calculator automatically know the correct VAT rate?

No. You enter the VAT rate yourself. The legally applicable rate can depend on the country, type of goods or services, customer, transaction, and tax rules in effect at that time.

What happens if the VAT rate is 0%?

A 0% rate produces no added VAT:

VAT = 0

and:

Gross Price = Net Price
Can I use any currency?

Yes. The calculation itself is currency-neutral. Changing the currency symbol changes how the result is displayed; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

Why might my invoice show a slightly different VAT amount?

Real invoices may apply jurisdiction-specific rounding methods or calculate VAT separately for individual line items. This can create small differences compared with calculating VAT once on a total amount.

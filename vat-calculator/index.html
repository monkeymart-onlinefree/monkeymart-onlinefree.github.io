What Is a VAT Calculator?

A VAT calculator converts between a net price, VAT amount, and gross price using a user-supplied VAT rate. It can add VAT to a tax-exclusive amount or extract the VAT already included in a tax-inclusive total.

How to Use the VAT Calculator

Choose Add VAT when you know the price before VAT. Choose Remove VAT when you know the VAT-inclusive total. Enter the applicable rate for your jurisdiction and transaction.

VAT Formulas

To add VAT: gross = net × (1 + rate). To remove VAT: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate). VAT is the difference between gross and net. The VAT fraction of a gross amount is rate ÷ (1 + rate).

Example

At a 20% rate, a net price of £100 becomes £120 gross and contains £20 VAT. If £120 is already VAT-inclusive, dividing by 1.20 returns the £100 net amount.

Limitations

VAT rates and exemptions vary by country, product, customer status, place of supply, and date. This calculator does not determine which VAT rate legally applies or whether a supply is exempt, zero-rated, reverse-charged, or outside scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can’t I remove 20% VAT by subtracting 20% from the gross price?

Because the 20% rate is applied to the smaller net price, not the larger gross price. To remove 20% VAT, divide the gross amount by 1.20; the VAT portion of the gross is one-sixth, or about 16.67%.

Is VAT the same as sales tax?

Both are consumption taxes, but VAT is generally collected through multiple stages of the supply chain with input-tax credits, while retail sales tax is typically collected at the final sale. Exact systems vary by country.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. The arithmetic is currency-neutral. The currency symbol field only changes the display; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

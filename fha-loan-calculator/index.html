Use the FHA Loan Calculator to estimate a monthly FHA mortgage payment from the home price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, mortgage insurance, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA fees. The calculation also finances the entered upfront mortgage insurance premium into the loan balance.

What Is the FHA Loan Calculator?

The FHA Loan Calculator estimates the monthly cost of a fixed-rate FHA-style mortgage. It first calculates the base loan after the down payment, adds the upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP) to create the financed loan amount, calculates monthly principal and interest, and then adds estimated monthly MIP, property tax, homeowners insurance, and HOA fees.

It is useful when comparing home prices, down payments, interest rates, and FHA mortgage-insurance assumptions before requesting an official lender quote.

How to Use the FHA Loan Calculator

  1. Home price: Enter the purchase price of the property in dollars.
  2. Down payment (%): Enter the percentage of the home price you plan to pay upfront.
  3. Interest rate (%): Enter the annual mortgage interest rate.
  4. Term (years): Enter the repayment term of the mortgage.
  5. Property tax (%/yr): Enter the estimated annual property-tax rate as a percentage of the home price.
  6. Home insurance ($/yr): Enter the estimated annual homeowners-insurance premium.
  7. HOA ($/month): Enter monthly homeowners-association dues. Use 0 if none apply.
  8. Upfront MIP (%): Enter the upfront FHA mortgage-insurance rate that applies to the loan.
  9. Annual MIP (%): Enter the annual mortgage-insurance rate applicable to the loan.

FHA rates and eligibility rules can change, so time-sensitive MIP values should be checked against current HUD guidance before using the estimate for planning.

Formula / Calculation Method

Main Formula

The calculator first converts the down payment percentage into dollars:

Down Payment = Home Price × Down Payment Rate

The base loan is:

Base Loan = Home Price − Down Payment

The upfront mortgage insurance premium is calculated as:

Upfront MIP = Base Loan × Upfront MIP Rate

The calculator assumes the upfront MIP is financed:

Financed Loan = Base Loan + Upfront MIP

Monthly principal and interest are calculated with the standard fixed-rate amortized-loan formula:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

The estimated total monthly housing payment is then:

Estimated Monthly Payment = P&I + Monthly MIP + Monthly Property Tax + Monthly Insurance + HOA

Variables

Symbol Meaning Unit
P Financed loan amount, including financed upfront MIP Dollars
r Monthly interest rate = annual interest rate ÷ 12 Decimal per month
n Total number of monthly payments = years × 12 Months
M Monthly principal-and-interest payment Dollars/month
B Base loan after down payment Dollars
u Upfront MIP rate Decimal
a Annual MIP rate Decimal
t Annual property-tax rate Decimal

Calculation Sequence

  1. Convert the down payment percentage into a dollar amount.
  2. Subtract the down payment from the home price to calculate the base loan.
  3. Multiply the base loan by the upfront MIP percentage.
  4. Add the upfront MIP to the base loan to determine the financed loan amount.
  5. Divide the annual interest rate by 12 to get the monthly rate.
  6. Multiply the loan term in years by 12 to get the number of monthly payments.
  7. Apply the fixed-rate mortgage formula to calculate monthly principal and interest.
  8. Calculate the monthly MIP, property-tax, and homeowners-insurance estimates.
  9. Add those costs and monthly HOA fees to principal and interest.

Down Payment and Base Loan

Down Payment = Home Price × Down Payment %
Base Loan = Home Price − Down Payment

For example, a 3.5% down payment on a $400,000 home equals $14,000, leaving a $386,000 base loan.

Upfront Mortgage Insurance Premium

The calculator uses:

Upfront MIP = Base Loan × Upfront MIP Rate

and then:

Financed Loan = Base Loan + Upfront MIP

HUD's currently published FHA guidance lists a standard UFMIP of 1.75% of the base loan amount for most forward purchase and refinance mortgages.

Monthly MIP Estimate

CalcPro uses a simplified first-year estimate:

Monthly MIP Estimate = Base Loan × Annual MIP Rate ÷ 12

This is useful for payment planning, but it is not the full FHA premium-billing procedure. HUD's official periodic MIP calculation uses the annual average outstanding balance and defined rounding steps, so an actual lender's monthly MIP can differ from this simplified estimate.

Property Tax

Monthly Property Tax = Home Price × Annual Property Tax Rate ÷ 12

The property-tax percentage is supplied by the user. Actual tax bills depend on the property and local taxing authority.

Homeowners Insurance

Monthly Insurance = Annual Home Insurance ÷ 12

HOA Fees

The entered monthly HOA amount is added directly to the estimated monthly housing cost.

CFPB guidance similarly distinguishes principal and interest from the additional costs that can make up a total monthly housing payment, including mortgage insurance, property taxes, and homeowners insurance.

Worked Example

Assume the following values:

Inputs

  • Home price: $400,000
  • Down payment: 3.5%
  • Interest rate: 6.25%
  • Loan term: 30 years
  • Property tax: 1.20% per year
  • Home insurance: $1,800 per year
  • HOA: $100 per month
  • Upfront MIP: 1.75%
  • Annual MIP: 0.55%

The 0.55% annual MIP used in this example matches HUD's currently published rate for a mortgage over 15 years with a base loan of $726,200 or less and an LTV above 95%.

Step 1: Calculate the Base Loan

Down Payment = $400,000 × 0.035 = $14,000
Base Loan = $400,000 − $14,000 = $386,000

Step 2: Add Upfront MIP

Upfront MIP = $386,000 × 0.0175 = $6,755
Financed Loan = $386,000 + $6,755 = $392,755

Step 3: Calculate Principal and Interest

Monthly interest rate:

r = 0.0625 ÷ 12 = 0.0052083333

Number of payments:

n = 30 × 12 = 360

Using the amortized-loan formula:

M = $392,755 × [0.0052083333(1 + 0.0052083333)^360] ÷ [(1 + 0.0052083333)^360 − 1]
Monthly P&I ≈ $2,418.26

Step 4: Calculate Monthly MIP

Monthly MIP = $386,000 × 0.0055 ÷ 12
Monthly MIP ≈ $176.92

Step 5: Calculate Other Monthly Costs

Property tax:

$400,000 × 0.012 ÷ 12 = $400.00

Homeowners insurance:

$1,800 ÷ 12 = $150.00

HOA:

$100.00

Final Result

Estimated Monthly Payment = $2,418.26 + $176.92 + $400 + $150 + $100
Estimated Monthly Payment ≈ $3,245.18

This is an estimate based on the calculator's inputs and simplified MIP method. It is not an FHA approval or lender payment quote.

Understanding Your Results

The main result represents the estimated combined monthly housing cost generated from the values entered into the calculator.

Base loan is the home price remaining after the down payment.

Financed loan amount is the base loan plus the entered upfront MIP because this calculator assumes the upfront premium is financed.

Principal and interest represents the fixed monthly amount needed to amortize the financed loan over the selected term at the entered interest rate. CFPB explains that fixed-rate mortgage principal-and-interest payments are determined by the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment term.

Monthly MIP is CalcPro's simplified estimate based on the entered annual MIP rate.

Property tax and insurance convert the entered annual estimates into monthly amounts.

HOA fees are included as a monthly housing cost but may be billed separately from the mortgage payment.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator assumes a fixed interest rate and regular monthly payments over the full selected term. It assumes the entered upfront MIP is financed into the mortgage rather than paid separately at closing.

The annual MIP portion is a simplified first-year estimate based on the base loan and entered annual MIP percentage. FHA's actual monthly premium calculation can use the average outstanding loan balance and specific premium-calculation and rounding procedures.

Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA fees are treated as constant estimates. Real costs can change over time.

The calculator does not determine FHA eligibility or check credit qualifications, debt-to-income requirements, county-specific FHA loan limits, property eligibility, FHA case-number dates, streamline-refinance exceptions, lender overlays, closing costs, discount points, prepaid expenses, or other lender fees.

A lender's official Loan Estimate may therefore differ from the result.

Current FHA Rules and Data

Current-data check: September 24, 2026

FHA Down Payment and Maximum Financing

HUD's current published Handbook states that borrowers with a Minimum Decision Credit Score of 580 or higher are eligible for maximum FHA financing, while borrowers with scores from 500 to 579 are limited to a maximum 90% LTV. For standard purchase transactions, maximum financing is 96.5% LTV, corresponding to a 3.5% minimum investment when that maximum financing applies.

These rules are important context only. The CalcPro FHA Loan Calculator does not collect a credit score and does not determine whether a borrower qualifies.

FHA Upfront MIP

HUD's currently published FHA Handbook Appendix 1.0 lists:

Upfront MIP: 1.75% of the base loan amount

for most FHA forward mortgages, subject to program-specific exceptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this FHA Loan Calculator determine whether I qualify for an FHA loan?

No. It estimates payment amounts only. It does not check credit score, income, debt-to-income ratio, property eligibility, county loan limits, lender underwriting requirements, or other FHA qualification rules.

Does the calculator automatically choose my FHA MIP rate?

The calculator includes upfront MIP and annual MIP as inputs. Use the rates that apply to your mortgage scenario and verify time-sensitive FHA premium rules with current HUD guidance.

Why does the calculator finance upfront MIP?

The implemented calculation adds the entered upfront MIP to the base loan before calculating principal and interest. This models a loan where the upfront premium is financed rather than paid separately at closing.

Why can my lender's FHA payment be different?

A lender may use FHA's official periodic MIP calculation, exact escrow amounts, property-specific taxes, insurance premiums, loan-level terms, rounding rules, and other costs that are not fully modeled by this calculator.

Does FHA annual MIP always last for the entire mortgage?

No. Under HUD's currently published premium structure, duration depends on the original LTV and loan term. Some loans have MIP for 11 years, while others have it for the mortgage term.

Does this calculator include closing costs?

No. Closing costs, lender fees, discount points, appraisal costs, prepaid expenses, and other transaction costs are not among the calculator's listed inputs and are not included in the monthly estimate.

Are property taxes and insurance part of principal and interest?

No. Principal and interest repay the mortgage itself. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage insurance are separate costs that may be included in the amount collected with a monthly mortgage payment.

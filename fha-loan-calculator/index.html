What Is an FHA Loan Calculator?

An FHA loan calculator estimates the monthly cost of an FHA-insured mortgage. It combines principal and interest with FHA mortgage insurance and optional ownership costs such as property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues.

Current FHA Mortgage Insurance Inputs

HUD states that most FHA forward purchase and refinance mortgages use a 1.75% upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP). Annual MIP depends on the original loan term, base loan amount, and loan-to-value ratio. This calculator can auto-fill the current HUD tier or let you override the rate for a lender-specific scenario.

Base Loan = Home Price − Down Payment
Financed Loan = Base Loan + Upfront MIP
Monthly P&I = standard fixed-rate amortization payment

Down Payment and LTV

HUD program information describes a typical minimum cash investment of 3.5% for qualifying FHA purchase financing. A lower down-payment entry in this calculator is allowed for scenario testing, but it does not mean the loan would be eligible.

Understanding Annual MIP Duration

For many current forward mortgages, annual MIP generally runs for 11 years when original LTV is 90% or less and for the mortgage term when original LTV exceeds 90%. The calculator displays that standard duration based on the values entered.

Limitations

This tool does not check FHA loan limits, credit qualifications, debt-to-income rules, property eligibility, case-number date, streamline-refinance exceptions, or lender overlays. Actual monthly MIP billing can also use FHA’s premium calculation procedures rather than a simple first-year estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA upfront MIP?

HUD states that the upfront mortgage insurance premium for most FHA forward purchase and refinance mortgages is 1.75% of the base loan amount. It is commonly financed into the mortgage.

How long does annual FHA MIP last?

For many current FHA forward mortgages, annual MIP generally lasts 11 years when the original LTV is 90% or less and for the mortgage term when original LTV is above 90%. Program exceptions exist.

Does this calculator determine FHA eligibility?

No. It estimates payment components only. Actual FHA qualification also depends on borrower, property, loan-limit, underwriting, and program requirements.

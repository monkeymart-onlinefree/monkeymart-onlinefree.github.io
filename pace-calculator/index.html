What Is a Pace Calculator?

Answer: A pace calculator connects distance, time, and pace. Pace is the amount of time required to cover one unit of distance, such as minutes per kilometer or minutes per mile.

How to Use the Pace Calculator

  1. Choose whether to calculate pace, time, or distance.
  2. Enter the other two known values.
  3. Select miles or kilometers where relevant.
  4. Calculate and compare pace and speed conversions.

Pace Formula

Pace = Time ÷ Distance Time = Pace × Distance Distance = Time ÷ Pace

Calculation Example

Running 5 km in 25 minutes gives a pace of 5:00 per kilometer, equivalent to about 8:03 per mile.

Assumptions and Limitations

This calculator assumes average pace across the full distance. Real-world pace varies with terrain, stops, elevation, weather, and measurement accuracy.

Pace Calculator FAQs

What is pace?

Pace is time per unit distance, commonly minutes per kilometer or minutes per mile.

How is pace different from speed?

Pace expresses time per distance; speed expresses distance per time.

Can I use this for walking or cycling?

Yes. The arithmetic applies to any activity where time and distance are known.

The Pace Calculator helps you calculate running or walking pace, total time, or distance using miles or kilometers. Enter the known distance, time, or pace values to find the missing value, along with speed and an equivalent pace in the other common distance unit.

What Is the Pace Calculator?

A pace calculator measures how much time it takes to cover a unit of distance, such as minutes per mile or minutes per kilometer.

You can use it to:

  • Find your average pace from distance and total time.
  • Estimate total time from a known pace and distance.
  • Find distance from pace and elapsed time.
  • Compare min/mile and min/km pace.
  • View the corresponding average speed.

It is useful for runners and walkers who want a simple way to compare pace, distance, and time.

How to Use the Pace Calculator

Distance

Enter the distance covered or planned.

Use the distance unit supported by the calculator, such as:

  • Miles
  • Kilometers

Distance is required when calculating pace or total time.

Time

Enter the total elapsed time for the distance.

Time represents how long it took, or is expected to take, to cover the selected distance.

Pace

Enter your average pace when you want to calculate total time or distance.

Pace is expressed as time per unit of distance, such as:

  • min/mile
  • min/km

Use the fields for the values you already know. The calculator applies the appropriate relationship between distance, time, and pace to determine the missing value.

Formula / Calculation Method

The calculator uses the relationship between pace, time, and distance.

Main Formula

To calculate pace:

P=TDP=\frac{T}{D}

To calculate time:

T=P×DT=P\times D

To calculate distance:

D=TPD=\frac{T}{P}

Variables

  • P = average pace, measured as time per mile or time per kilometer
  • T = total elapsed time
  • D = total distance in miles or kilometers

Pace is therefore the amount of elapsed time required to cover one unit of distance.

Calculation Sequence

  1. Read the entered distance, time, and pace values.
  2. Convert time into a consistent calculation unit where needed.
  3. Use the two known values to calculate pace, time, or distance.
  4. Calculate the corresponding average speed.
  5. Convert the pace into the equivalent min/mile or min/km value when required.
  6. Display the results using the calculator's output units.

The relationship between distance and elapsed time is consistent with the standard definition of average speed as distance divided by time.

Speed

Average speed follows:

v=DTv=\frac{D}{T}

where:

  • v = average speed
  • D = distance
  • T = elapsed time

For example, if distance is measured in kilometers and time is converted to hours, the resulting speed is in kilometers per hour.

Equivalent Pace

The calculator can express the same pace in both minutes per kilometer and minutes per mile.

NIST defines:

1 mile=1.609344 km1\text{ mile}=1.609344\text{ km}

Therefore:

min/mile=min/km×1.609344\text{min/mile}=\text{min/km}\times1.609344

and:

min/km=min/mile1.609344\text{min/km}=\frac{\text{min/mile}}{1.609344}

The mile-to-kilometer conversion factor is exact.

Worked Example

Suppose a runner completes 10 kilometers in 50 minutes.

Inputs

  • Distance = 10 km
  • Time = 50 minutes

Step 1: Calculate Pace

P=TDP=\frac{T}{D} P=5010P=\frac{50}{10} P=5 minutes per kilometerP=5\text{ minutes per kilometer}

The average pace is:

5:00 min/km

Step 2: Calculate Average Speed

Convert 50 minutes to hours:

50÷60=0.833333 hours50\div60=0.833333\text{ hours}

Then:

v=100.833333v=\frac{10}{0.833333} v=12 km/hv=12\text{ km/h}

Step 3: Convert to Minutes per Mile

5×1.609344=8.04672 min/mile5\times1.609344=8.04672\text{ min/mile}

Convert the decimal part to seconds:

0.04672×60=2.8032 seconds0.04672\times60=2.8032\text{ seconds}

Equivalent pace is approximately:

8:03 min/mile

Final Result

For 10 km completed in 50 minutes:

  • Pace: 5:00 min/km
  • Average speed: 12 km/h
  • Equivalent pace: approximately 8:03 min/mile

Small differences may appear when converted results are rounded for display.

Understanding Your Results

Pace

Pace tells you how much time you take to travel one mile or one kilometer.

A result of 6:00 min/km, for example, means an average of six minutes is required for each kilometer.

Time

Time is the total duration required to cover the entered distance at the given average pace.

The calculation assumes that the entered pace represents the average pace across the complete distance.

Distance

Distance shows how far you would travel during the entered amount of time at the specified average pace.

Speed

Speed expresses distance traveled per unit of time, such as kilometers per hour or miles per hour. Average speed is calculated from total distance divided by total elapsed time.

Equivalent Pace

Equivalent pace expresses the same movement rate using another distance unit. For example, a pace entered in min/km can be shown as its corresponding min/mile pace.

Assumptions and Limitations

The calculator uses average pace across the full distance. It does not model changes in pace during individual sections of a run or walk.

Actual pace can vary because of:

  • Terrain
  • Hills and elevation
  • Stops or breaks
  • Weather
  • GPS or distance-measurement accuracy
  • Changes in effort during the activity

When the calculator estimates future time or distance from a pace, it assumes that the entered average pace can be maintained for the calculated distance or duration.

The pace formula itself does not depend on age or sex. It is a mathematical relationship between distance and elapsed time and should not be interpreted as a measure of health, fitness level, or recommended exercise intensity.

Distance and pace values used as divisors must also be greater than zero for the corresponding calculation to produce a meaningful result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is running pace calculated?

Running pace is calculated by dividing total elapsed time by total distance:

Pace=TimeDistancePace=\frac{Time}{Distance}

For example, 30 minutes divided by 5 km equals a pace of 6 minutes per kilometer.

Can I calculate total time from pace?

Yes. Multiply pace by distance:

Time=Pace×DistanceTime=Pace\times Distance

A pace of 5 min/km over 10 km gives a total time of 50 minutes.

Can the calculator find distance from time and pace?

Yes. Divide total time by pace:

Distance=TimePaceDistance=\frac{Time}{Pace}

The time and pace units must be compatible for the result to be meaningful.

What is the difference between pace and speed?

Pace measures time per unit of distance, such as min/km. Speed measures distance per unit of time, such as km/h.

They describe the same movement in different ways.

How do I convert min/km to min/mile?

Multiply the min/km pace by 1.609344 because one mile equals exactly 1.609344 kilometers.

For example:

5 min/km×1.609344=8.04672 min/mile5\text{ min/km}\times1.609344=8.04672\text{ min/mile}

which is approximately 8:03 min/mile.

Why can my actual finish time differ from the calculator result?

The calculation uses average pace. Real-world pace can change because of hills, fatigue, weather, stops, route conditions, and inaccurate distance measurements.

Is pace a medical or fitness assessment?

No. This calculator only performs pace, time, distance, speed, and unit calculations. It does not diagnose health conditions or determine an individually appropriate exercise intensity.

What Is a One Rep Max Calculator?

A one rep max calculator estimates the maximum load you may be able to lift for one repetition of a specific exercise, based on a submaximal set performed for a known number of repetitions.

1RM Estimation Formulas

Epley: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)
Brzycki: 1RM = weight ÷ (1.0278 − 0.0278 × reps)
Lombardi: 1RM = weight × reps^0.10

How to Use the Estimate

Use a technically sound set and enter the load and completed repetitions. Calculator.net recommends using a weight lifted between 1 and 10 times for its estimator. The result is exercise-specific: a bench press estimate does not transfer directly to a squat or deadlift.

Safety and Limitations

A formula is not a guarantee that a maximal attempt is safe or achievable. Fatigue, technique, training experience, exercise selection, and individual differences matter. Beginners and people with health concerns should avoid unsupervised maximal lifting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a one rep max?

One-repetition maximum (1RM) is the greatest load a person can lift once for a specific exercise with acceptable technique.

Why should I use fewer reps for a 1RM estimate?

Prediction equations generally become less dependable as repetitions increase. A lower-repetition set close to maximal effort is usually more informative than a high-repetition endurance set.

Do all 1RM formulas give the same answer?

No. Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, and other equations were derived differently, so estimates can vary. This calculator lets you compare multiple methods.
